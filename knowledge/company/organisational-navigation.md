# Organisational Navigation and Knowledge Sources

## 1 Who owns what
- Engineering: product design, design-for-manufacturing, engineering change notices (ECN).
- NPD (New Product Development): programme launch and APQP leadership from nomination to SOP.
- Quality: customer quality, complaint containment, 8D, PPAP submissions.
- SCM (Supply Chain Management): production planning, customer schedules, supplier risk, premium-freight approval (Head of SCM).
- Plant: production, capacity, OEE, delivery performance (OTIF).
- Finance: cost models, overhead rates, margin targets, credit and payment-term analysis.

## 2 How to navigate
Start with the owner of the decision, not the most senior person. Bring facts and a clear ask. Use the weekly internal account review for cross-functional issues.

## 3 Knowledge sources
Approved SOPs, policies and guides are held in the Compass knowledge base with version and effective date. Customer data comes only from approved Customer 360 records. If a source is not approved or is out of date, do not rely on it — ask the document owner. The Ask FirstGear assistant answers only from approved sources and shows the source of every answer.
