# FirstGear Company Overview

## 1 Who we are
FirstGear Components Ltd (synthetic company for this programme) is an automotive-components manufacturer founded in 1998, supplying passenger-car, SUV, LCV and two-wheeler OEMs from six plants.

## 2 Strategy
Grow the BEV and hybrid share of revenue to 40% by 2030 while protecting ICE profitability, deepen relationships with five strategic OEM accounts, and lead on quality (customer PPM below 25).

## 3 Values
Customer first; Integrity; Ownership; Safety always; Continuous improvement.

## 4 How values show up in key account work
Customer first means telling the customer bad news early with a plan. Integrity means never committing what has not been approved. Ownership means following an action to closure across functions.
