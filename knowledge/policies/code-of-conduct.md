# Code of Conduct

## 1 Gifts and hospitality
Employees may not offer or accept gifts or hospitality that could influence a business decision. Gifts above a nominal value of ₹5,000 must be declared to Compliance and are normally declined. Cash or cash equivalents are never acceptable.

## 2 Anti-bribery
No payment, favour or benefit may be offered to a customer employee to win or keep business.

## 3 Competition law
Never discuss prices, margins, capacity or bidding intentions with competitors, including at industry events. Leave any conversation that turns to these topics and report it to Legal.

## 4 Confidentiality
Customer drawings, prices and programme information are confidential. Share them only with colleagues who need them for their work, and never with other customers.

## 5 Speaking up
Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR or the confidential ethics line without fear of retaliation.
