# KAM FAQ — Company, Products and Markets

Questions a Key Account Manager is most often asked about FirstGear itself. FirstGear is the training alias of an NSE/BSE-listed mobility-technology company; figures are as publicly reported for FY 2025-26 (April 2025 – March 2026) and Q1 FY 2026-27. Detailed figures: FirstGear Company Overview and Product Portfolio Guide.

### Q: How do I describe FirstGear to a customer in one line?
A mobility-technology company that designs and manufactures mission-critical driveline systems, electric motors and controllers, radar sensors and railway systems for global OEMs, with a leading global position in differential gears. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §1.

### Q: Where is FirstGear headquartered and where does it manufacture?
Headquarters is Gurugram, Haryana. There are 12 manufacturing and assembly facilities across India, the USA, Mexico and China. Indian plants (Gurugram, Manesar, Pune/Chakan, Chennai and the railway plant) manufacture; the US, Mexico and China sites mainly do final assembly and finishing close to customers. Source: Manufacturing Process Overview §1.

### Q: How big is FirstGear?
FY 2025-26 revenue was INR 44,751 mn (+26%), EBITDA INR 11,069 mn (24.7% margin) and adjusted profit after tax INR 6,703 mn. The workforce was 6,691 people. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §1 and §6.

### Q: How has revenue grown over the last few years?
Revenue was INR 26,756 mn in FY 2022-23, 31,848 mn in FY 2023-24, 35,545 mn in FY 2024-25 and 44,751 mn in FY 2025-26. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §6.

### Q: Why did the EBITDA margin fall in FY 2025-26?
It fell from 27.4% to 24.7%. The company reports that material cost rose from 43% to 47% of total income because of the change in product mix. In Q1 FY 2026-27 the margin was 23.1%; management cited commodity and labour costs, a mix shift to lower-margin traction motors and a lag in passing costs to customers. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §6.

### Q: What does FirstGear make?
Four families: driveline (differential bevel gears, differential assemblies and other gears), motors and controllers (starter motors, BSG, EV traction motors, controllers, active-suspension motor controllers), sensors (in-cabin and short-range radar) and railway systems (brakes, couplers, dampers, HVAC, electric panels). Robotics and eVTOL parts are emerging. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §1–5.

### Q: What is FirstGear's market position?
Global market share of 8.7% in differential gears and 4.2% in starter motors in calendar 2025. 500 million differential gears and 10 million differential assemblies had been produced by April 2026. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §1–2.

### Q: How much of the business is electric vehicles?
BEV revenue was INR 11,542 mn in FY 2025-26 — 35% of automotive product revenue (36% the year before). In Q1 FY 2026-27 it reached an all-time high of 44%. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §7.

### Q: How many EV programmes and customers does FirstGear have?
67 EV programmes across 35 customers at 31 March 2026, rising to 69 programmes across 36 customers by June 2026. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §7.

### Q: What is the order book and what does "net order book" mean?
INR 237 bn (about USD 2.5 bn) at 31 March 2026, around 70% EV; about INR 240 bn by June 2026. It is the revenue expected over the next 10 years from awarded programmes not yet started or fully ramped, net of programmes ending, with a discount for possible launch delays; it includes the railway order book. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §7.

### Q: Which markets does FirstGear sell into?
In FY 2025-26 India was the largest end market at 51% of revenue (29% the year before); 49% of sales are international. In Q1 FY 2026-27: India 50%, Europe 26%, North America 15%, rest of Asia 9%. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §8.

### Q: What is FirstGear's strategy?
Four priorities: electrification, global market significance, diversification (geography, product, segment) and technology (new verticals in sensors, controllers, railways, robotics and eVTOL). Source: FirstGear Company Overview §5.

### Q: What are the company values and what do they mean for a KAM?
Integrity, Vitality, Frugality and Agility. For a KAM: integrity — never commit what has not been approved and share bad news early with a plan; vitality — bring better solutions; frugality — challenge cost before conceding price; agility — respond fast and reliably. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §3–4.

### Q: Which products belong to ICE, hybrid and BEV?
ICE: differential gears and assemblies and conventional starter motors. Hybrid: micro-hybrid starter motors, belt starter generators (BSG) and hybrid-platform differential assemblies. BEV: EV differential assemblies with final-drive gear, EV transmission gears, traction motors, motor controllers and suspension motors. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §6.

### Q: Why do EV gears and differentials need tighter tolerances?
An electric drivetrain has no engine noise to mask gear whine, so EV gears and differentials must meet tighter noise-vibration-harshness (NVH) and precision requirements. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §6.

### Q: Which traction motors does FirstGear supply?
BLDC and PMSM traction motors, hub-wheel motors and integrated drive motor-controller units, mainly for electric two- and three-wheelers in India. A rare-earth-free synchronous reluctance motor was developed in FY 2025-26. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §2.

### Q: What is the integrated motor controller module for active suspension?
A new product line for predictive active suspension systems; INR 8.2 bn of nominations were won in FY 2025-26 from an Asian EV OEM and a European luxury performance OEM. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §2.

### Q: What did the railway acquisition add?
Railway brake systems, couplers, dampers, suspension and friction products, plus HVAC systems and electric panels commercialised in FY 2025-26. Together with off-highway growth it lifted non-automotive revenue from 9% to 31% of revenue. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §4 and FirstGear Company Overview §8.

### Q: What are FirstGear's radar sensors for?
In-cabin and short-range radar sensors for occupant and surroundings detection, supporting safety and ADAS requirements. In-cabin radar sample production started at the Chennai SMT facility in FY 2025-26. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §3.

### Q: What are the biggest risks to the business?
Geopolitical and tariff uncertainty, supply disruptions (rare-earth magnets, electronics, special steels), raw-material price volatility, regulatory change and the ICE-to-EV transition for ICE-dependent lines. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §10.

### Q: How did FirstGear handle the 2025 rare-earth magnet export controls?
Engineering and operations redesigned motor architectures with customers and suppliers within four weeks so that customer schedules were not affected. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §4 and §10.

### Q: What should I say if a customer asks about our financial strength?
Share only published facts: the company was net cash (INR 10,262 mn at 31 March 2026) after funding the railway acquisition, with ROCE of 15.4%. Do not share unpublished or forward-looking numbers. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §6 and Code of Conduct §4.

### Q: What ESG facts can I share with customers?
Certifications ISO 14001, ISO 50001 and ISO 45001; zero lost-time injury frequency rate in FY 2025-26; water intensity down 25%; captive solar contracted in Haryana and Maharashtra; CRISIL ESG score 62 ("Strong"), 2nd of 56 listed auto-component companies. Source: FirstGear Company Overview §11.

### Q: Which vehicle segments do we serve?
Passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, buses, off-highway (tractors and recreational off-highway), electric two- and three-wheelers, railways and emerging robotics. Source: Product Portfolio Guide §7.

### Q: Can I use these company figures in a customer presentation?
Yes — they are published figures. Always quote the period (for example "FY 2025-26") and use the latest approved company presentation from Corporate Communications. Never share customer-specific or confidential numbers. Source: Organisational Navigation §3 and Code of Conduct §4.
