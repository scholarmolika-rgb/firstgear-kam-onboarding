# Customer 360 — Northwind Motors (synthetic account)

## 1 Organisation
Northwind Motors is a synthetic mid-size OEM used for training. Purchasing is organised by commodity: the powertrain commodity buyer handles FirstGear parts and reports to the Head of Powertrain Purchasing. Supplier Quality Engineering (SQE) is site-based at the Pune assembly plant.

## 2 Strategic context
Northwind targets a 30% electric-vehicle share of its volumes by 2030 and is consolidating its powertrain supplier base from nine to five suppliers. Suppliers are scored quarterly on quality (PPM), delivery (OTIF), cost competitiveness and EV readiness.

## 3 Supplied parts and applications
FirstGear supplies transmission shafts (ICE SUV platform), electric water-pump housings (hybrid) and e-axle gear sets (BEV compact SUV, in development).

## 4 Programmes and volumes
- Project Aster — BEV compact SUV, e-axle gear sets; nomination received; SOP planned for April 2027; peak volume 60,000 vehicles per year.
- Current SUV platform — transmission shafts; about 120,000 vehicles per year, declining from 2028.
- Hybrid sedan — water-pump housings; about 35,000 vehicles per year.

## 5 Pipeline
Open RFQ for battery cooling plates on the next-generation BEV platform (decision expected Q1 2027).

## 6 Pricing and commercial history
Annual productivity price-down of 2% agreed for 2025. Steel index adjustment applied in Q2 2026. Payment terms are 60 days. A premium-freight claim from 2025 was settled 50/50 after a delivery miss caused by a supplier fire.

## 7 Open commitments
8D closure for complaint NW-Q-2026-014 (burr on transmission shaft) due 30 days from the complaint date; PPAP submission for Project Aster gear sets planned for January 2027.

## 8 Lessons learned
Northwind values early warning: the 2025 delivery miss damaged trust mainly because it was communicated late. Always confirm dates with SCM in writing before sharing them.
