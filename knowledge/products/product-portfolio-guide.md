# Product Portfolio Guide

FirstGear (training alias of an NSE/BSE-listed mobility-technology company) supplies driveline systems, electric motors and controllers, radar sensors and railway systems. Products, market shares and revenue shares below are as publicly reported in the company's Annual Report 2025-26; products marked "(in development)" are reported as under development, not yet in series supply.

## 1 Driveline systems
The company's founding business, built on precision-forged ("near-net-shape") bevel-gear technology.
- Differential bevel gears — the core product; 8.7% global market share in differential gears (calendar 2025). 500 million differential gears produced by April 2026.
- Differential assemblies — complete differentials supplied ready to fit; 10 million produced by April 2026. For EVs these are supplied as differential assemblies with the final-drive gear.
- Other driveline gears and parts: spool gears, intermediate gears, epicyclic gear trains, inter-axle gear sets, input/rotor shafts, park gears, spiral bevel gears, portal-axle gears (buses), steering bevel box (in development), electronically locking differential (EDL, in development) and limited-slip differential (LSD, in development).
- Applications: passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, buses, off-highway vehicles (tractors and recreational off-highway) and electric light commercial vehicles / cargo three-wheelers.

## 2 Motors and controllers
Added in 2019 through the acquisition of an Indian motor maker.
- Conventional starter motors and micro-hybrid starter motors — 4.2% global market share in starter motors (calendar 2025).
- Belt starter generator (BSG) systems for mild hybrids.
- EV traction motors — BLDC (brushless DC) and PMSM (permanent-magnet synchronous) motors, mainly for electric two- and three-wheelers in India; 100,000 traction motors produced by 2022. Includes hub-wheel motors and integrated drive motor and controller units.
- Motor controllers (motor control units) for traction motors.
- Integrated motor controller module for predictive active suspension — a new product line; INR 8.2 bn of orders won in FY 2025-26 from an Asian EV OEM and a European luxury performance OEM.
- Hydraulic motor controller — commercialised in FY 2025-26 for electric farm equipment.
- Rare-earth-free synchronous reluctance motor for EVs — developed in FY 2025-26 to reduce dependence on rare-earth magnets.

## 3 Sensors and software
Added in 2023 through a majority stake in a Serbia-based radar-sensing company.
- In-cabin radar sensors (sample production started at the Chennai SMT facility in FY 2025-26).
- 360° short-range radar sensors (in development) and zone-monitoring sensors.
- Relevant to evolving safety and ADAS requirements, including those emerging for commercial vehicles in India.

## 4 Railway systems
Added in 2025 by acquiring the railway equipment division of an Indian engineering group.
- Brake systems: bogie-mounted, wheel-mounted disc, axle-mounted disc and electro-pneumatic brake systems; friction products.
- Couplers (AAR-H, automatic, semi-automatic and semi-permanent), dampers and suspension systems.
- Railway HVAC systems and electric control panels — both commercialised in FY 2025-26.

## 5 Non-automotive and robotics (emerging)
Industrial-robot gearboxes, AGV/AMR drive units, humanoid components and motors, and eVTOL motors and reduction gearboxes — largely in development. The company signed a collaboration with a German cognitive-robotics company for robots, cobots and humanoids, and an MoU with an Indian eVTOL developer for powertrains.

## 6 Mapping to powertrain technologies
- ICE (internal combustion): differential gears and assemblies and conventional starter motors. Non-BEV products still made up about 65% of automotive product revenue in FY 2025-26, and the company reports the ICE-to-EV transition as a risk to ICE-dependent lines.
- Hybrid: micro-hybrid starter motors, BSG systems and differential assemblies for hybrid platforms (a hybrid-platform programme was won in FY 2025-26; the company sees hybrids as an opportunity).
- BEV (battery electric): EV differential assemblies with final-drive gear, EV transmission gears, traction motors, motor controllers and suspension motors. BEV revenue was 35% of automotive product revenue in FY 2025-26 and 44% in Q1 FY 2026-27.
- What BEV parts demand (engineering note, not a company figure): an electric drivetrain has no engine noise to mask gear whine, so EV gears and differentials must meet tighter noise-vibration-harshness (NVH) and precision requirements; traction motors depend on magnets (hence rare-earth supply risk) and controllers.

## 7 Vehicle segments and end markets
Passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, buses, off-highway (tractors, recreational off-highway), electric two- and three-wheelers, railways and emerging robotics. In FY 2025-26 India was the largest end market (51% of revenue), and non-automotive revenue was 31%.

## 8 Customer problems each product line solves
- Driveline: efficient, durable and quiet torque transfer to the wheels (efficiency, NVH, durability, weight).
- Motors and controllers: reliable starting and start-stop for fuel saving (starter motors and BSG); efficient electric propulsion for EVs (traction motors and controllers); ride comfort (active suspension).
- Sensors: occupant and surroundings detection for safety and ADAS.
- Railway: safe braking and coupling, and passenger comfort (HVAC).
