# Product Portfolio Guide

FirstGear Components supplies precision-machined and assembled powertrain and thermal components across three technology families.

## 1 ICE technology family
Internal-combustion engine components: fuel-rail assemblies, valvetrain components (rocker arms, lash adjusters), oil-pump housings and transmission shafts. ICE remains the largest revenue family but is declining as OEMs electrify.

## 2 Hybrid technology family
Components for mild and full hybrids: start-stop components, electric water-pump housings, transmission parts for dedicated hybrid transmissions, and thermal-management valves. Hybrids reuse many ICE capabilities but add thermal and electrical integration requirements.

## 3 BEV technology family
Battery-electric vehicle components: e-axle gear sets and shafts, e-motor housings, battery cooling plates and thermal-management modules. BEV parts demand tighter noise-vibration (NVH) tolerances on gears and leak-tight cooling plates.

## 4 Vehicle segments
Passenger cars (hatchback and sedan), SUVs (the fastest-growing segment), light commercial vehicles (LCV) and two-wheelers (mainly ICE and emerging electric scooters).

## 5 Major applications
Powertrain (transmission, e-axle), engine (fuel, valvetrain, lubrication) and thermal management (battery and motor cooling). The KAM should be able to explain which customer problem each application solves: efficiency, NVH, durability and thermal stability.
