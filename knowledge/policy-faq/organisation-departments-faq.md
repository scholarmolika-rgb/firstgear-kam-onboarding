# Policy FAQ — Organisation Chart and Departments

The business structure (business lines, plants, R&D centres) follows FirstGear's publicly reported profile. Reporting lines, department responsibilities, contact routes and response times are an illustrative model organisation for the Compass training programme — check the live organisation chart in the HR portal for current names.

Key structure at a glance:
- Board of Directors with a non-executive Chairman. Managing Director and Group CEO leads the company.
- Group CFO (President): finance, costing, credit control, treasury, tax, accounts payable, investor relations support.
- Group CTO: engineering and R&D (five R&D centres), product development for driveline, motors, sensors and new verticals.
- Business and operations heads: Driveline, Motors, Sensors and Software, Railway; Plant Heads for each plant; Head of SCM; Head of Quality; Head of NPD/Programme Management.
- Head of Key Accounts (sales): the KAMs' Reporting Boss; Senior KAMs act as Mentors.
- Support functions: HR (CHRO), Administration, IT, Legal and Compliance (Company Secretary), Corporate Communications, EHS.

### Q: Who leads FirstGear?
The Managing Director and Group CEO leads the company and reports to the Board of Directors, which has a non-executive Chairman and independent directors, as required for a listed company. The leadership team includes the Group CFO, the Group CTO, the business and operations heads, the CHRO and the heads of key functions. Source: Organisation FAQ §1 Leadership.

### Q: Where does a KAM sit in the organisation?
KAMs report to the Head of Key Accounts (your Reporting Boss), who reports to the Managing Director and Group CEO. Senior KAMs act as Mentors for new KAMs. For each account, the KAM works with a cross-functional team from Engineering, NPD, Quality, SCM, the Plant and Finance. Source: Organisation FAQ §2 Key Accounts.

### Q: What are FirstGear's business lines?
Driveline (differential bevel gears, differential assemblies and other gears), Motors and controllers (starter motors, belt starter generators, EV traction motors, controllers), Sensors and Software (in-cabin and short-range radar) and Railway (brakes, couplers, dampers, HVAC, electric panels), plus emerging robotics and eVTOL work. Source: Organisation FAQ §3 Business lines.

### Q: Which plants and sites does FirstGear have?
Twelve manufacturing and assembly facilities across India, the USA, Mexico and China. In India: Gurugram (Units I–III) and Manesar for gears and differential assemblies; Pune and Chakan for gears; Chennai for starter and traction motors and the sensor SMT line; and railway equipment plant(s). Overseas sites in Michigan (USA), Mexico (including Silao) and China mainly do final assembly and finishing. Headquarters is in Gurugram. Source: Organisation FAQ §4 Sites.

### Q: What does the Head of Key Accounts do?
Owns the key-account strategy, AOP targets and pricing approvals at or above the margin floor (and adjustments within ±2%); is the Reporting Boss who approves progression, guided pricing and customer ownership for new KAMs and records the Day-30 sign-off; leads quarterly business reviews with customer leadership; handles Level-2 escalations. Source: Organisation FAQ §2 Key Accounts.

### Q: What does a Mentor do?
A Mentor is an experienced (usually G3) KAM assigned by HR to coach you during onboarding: running sessions, reviewing your Customer 360 and account brief, approving submitted work, certifying the Day-21 scenario test and giving panel input at Day 30. Your Mentor's name is on your Compass dashboard. Source: Organisation FAQ §2 Mentors.

### Q: What does Engineering do?
Engineering, under the Group CTO, owns product design, design-for-manufacturing (DFM), technical feasibility of RFQs, engineering change notices (ECN) and technical answers to customer questions. Five R&D centres develop driveline, motor, sensor and software technologies. Contact the account's application engineer first; escalate to the Engineering Manager. Source: Organisation FAQ §5 Engineering.

### Q: What does NPD (New Product Development) do?
NPD runs programme launches and APQP from nomination to start of production (SOP): timing plans, tooling, off-tool samples, PPAP preparation with Quality, and launch stabilisation for the first 90 days. Each programme has an NPD programme manager — your key partner from nomination to SOP. Source: Organisation FAQ §6 NPD.

### Q: What does Quality do?
Quality owns customer quality: complaint containment, 8D reports, PPAP submissions, customer-specific requirements, audits and supplier quality. Customer quality engineers are assigned per customer and plant. The KAM owns communication with the customer; Quality owns the technical content and dates. Source: Organisation FAQ §7 Quality.

### Q: What does SCM (Supply Chain Management) do?
SCM handles production planning, customer schedules and call-offs, procurement of materials, supplier risk, logistics, packaging and premium-freight approval (by the Head of SCM). Only dates confirmed in writing by SCM and the Plant may be given to customers. Source: Organisation FAQ §8 SCM.

### Q: What do the Plants do?
Each Plant Head is responsible for production, capacity, OEE, safety and delivery performance (OTIF) of their site. Plants confirm cycle times and capacity for RFQs and recovery plans for delivery risks. Customer visits to plants need the Plant Head's approval. Source: Organisation FAQ §9 Plants.

### Q: What does Finance do for a KAM?
Finance owns cost models, overhead rates and margin targets for quotations, account profitability, credit control and collections, customer credit limits, invoicing, credit and debit notes, payment-term analysis and tax. The cost controller reviews every quotation; the credit-control team runs the overdue list with you. Source: Organisation FAQ §10 Finance.

### Q: What does HR do?
HR (led by the CHRO) handles recruitment, onboarding and the Compass programme, payroll, benefits, performance management, learning, employee relations and exits. Each function has an HR Business Partner (HRBP). HR also gives the HR panel input at Day 30. Source: Organisation FAQ §11 HR.

### Q: What does Administration do?
Facilities, ID cards, assets (other than IT set-up), the travel desk, guest houses, visitor management, courier and mailroom, pool cars, canteen and transport. Requests go through the Admin helpdesk. Source: Organisation FAQ §12 Administration.

### Q: What does IT do?
IT provides laptops, email, the CRM and ERP access, VPN, the employee portal, information security and the IT helpdesk. Access requests are approved by your Reporting Boss and set up by IT; IT never asks for your password. Source: Organisation FAQ §13 IT.

### Q: What does Legal and Compliance do?
Reviews customer contracts, NDAs, terms and conditions, warranty and liability clauses, letters of intent; runs the ethics line, anti-bribery and competition-law programmes; and, through the Company Secretary, manages listed-company obligations such as the insider-trading code and disclosures. Source: Organisation FAQ §14 Legal and Compliance.

### Q: What does Corporate Communications do?
Owns the company's external communication, brand, presentations, media and investor communication with Investor Relations. Use only the approved corporate presentation with customers; never speak to media or analysts about the company or customers. Source: Organisation FAQ §15 Corporate Communications.

### Q: What does EHS do?
Environment, Health and Safety runs safety inductions, PPE rules, incident and near-miss reporting, emergency preparedness and environmental compliance under ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and ISO 50001. Report any incident within 24 hours. Source: Organisation FAQ §16 EHS.

### Q: Who do I go to for a pricing question?
Prepare the analysis with the Finance cost controller; the Head of Key Accounts approves prices at or above the margin floor and adjustments within ±2%; the CFO approves below-floor prices, adjustments beyond ±2% and contracts above ₹50 crore lifetime value; the Managing Director approves strategic below-floor pricing. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: Who do I go to for a delivery problem?
The SCM planner for your customer first, together with the Plant production planner; escalate to the Head of SCM and Plant Head if the recovery plan is not agreed within 24 hours. Premium freight needs the Head of SCM's approval. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: Who do I go to for a quality complaint?
The customer quality engineer for that customer and plant; they start containment and the 8D. Escalate to the Plant Quality Head and then the Head of Quality if containment is not in place within 24 hours. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: Who do I go to for a technical question from the customer?
The application or product engineer for the account in Engineering. Do not answer technical questions yourself unless the answer is in an approved document; agree a response time with the customer and confirm the answer in writing. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: Who do I go to for a contract or NDA?
Legal and Compliance, through the legal request form in the employee portal, with the customer's draft. Standard NDAs are turned around within 3 working days; contract reviews within 10. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: Who do I go to for a payment dispute?
Finance's credit-control team for your customer, with the invoice numbers and the reason for the dispute; resolve root causes (price, quantity, quality debit) with the owning function. Source: Organisation FAQ §17 Who to ask.

### Q: How do escalations move up the organisation?
Level 1 — KAM and the responsible function lead (response within 24 hours). Level 2 — Head of Key Accounts and function heads (within 48 hours). Level 3 — Managing Director and Plant Head, for line-stop risk, safety issues or strategic relationship risk. Source: Organisation FAQ §18 Escalation.

### Q: Who is the right person to start with in another department?
The owner of the decision, not the most senior person. Bring the facts and a clear ask. Use the weekly internal account review for issues that need several functions. Source: Organisation FAQ §18 Navigation.

### Q: What is the internal account team?
For each key account, the KAM leads a virtual team: application engineer, NPD programme manager, customer quality engineer, SCM planner, plant representative and Finance cost controller. The team meets weekly in the internal account review chaired by the KAM. Source: Organisation FAQ §19 Account team.

### Q: How do I find someone's contact details?
Search the company directory in the employee portal or email address book. Role-based contacts (for example the customer quality engineer for a customer) are listed in the account's CRM record. Source: Organisation FAQ §20 Directory.

### Q: Where is the latest organisation chart?
In the HR portal under "Organisation", updated whenever HR records a change. The charts in presentations may be out of date — always check the portal. Source: Organisation FAQ §20 Directory.

### Q: Who approves my access to systems?
Your Reporting Boss approves CRM, ERP and shared-drive access requests raised in the IT helpdesk; data owners approve access to restricted folders (for example Finance cost models). Source: Organisation FAQ §13 IT.

### Q: Who decides my readiness at Day 30?
The Reporting Boss (Head of Key Accounts) records the final decision after the readiness panel with your Mentor and HR. Source: Organisation FAQ §2 Key Accounts.

### Q: Who manages the railway business relationship?
The Railway business has its own key-account and tender team because customers are mainly railway authorities and rolling-stock makers with tender-based buying. Automotive KAMs coordinate with them for any shared customer or plant capacity. Source: Organisation FAQ §3 Railway.

### Q: Who manages overseas customers?
Overseas customers are managed by KAMs in India with support from the overseas assembly sites for logistics and local presence. Export documentation and customs are handled by SCM logistics. Source: Organisation FAQ §21 International.

### Q: What is the role of the R&D centres for customers?
They develop new products and technologies (for example traction motors, controllers, radar sensors) and support customer advanced-engineering discussions. Requests for development work go through the application engineer and Engineering's project-intake process, not directly to R&D teams. Source: Organisation FAQ §5 R&D.

### Q: Who is the Company Secretary and why does it matter?
The Company Secretary manages listed-company compliance, including the insider-trading code (trading windows and pre-clearance for designated persons) and disclosure of price-sensitive information. KAMs handling large orders may be designated persons — see the Compliance and Ethics FAQ. Source: Organisation FAQ §14 Company Secretary.

### Q: Who handles investor or analyst questions?
Only Investor Relations and authorised spokespersons. If an investor, analyst or journalist contacts you, do not comment — refer them to Investor Relations or Corporate Communications. Source: Organisation FAQ §15 Investor Relations.

### Q: How many people work at FirstGear?
The company reported a total workforce of 6,691 at the end of FY 2025-26. Source: Organisation FAQ §22 Workforce.

### Q: What is a function head?
The leader of a department (for example the Head of Quality or Head of SCM). Function heads join Level-2 escalations, approve exceptions in their area and nominate members to account teams. Source: Organisation FAQ §18 Escalation.

### Q: Who do I contact for the Compass onboarding programme?
Your Mentor for learning and reviews, your Reporting Boss for decisions, and HR (the programme owner) for anything about the programme, schedule changes or access. Source: Organisation FAQ §11 HR.

### Q: What is a cost centre and which one am I in?
A cost centre is the unit where your salary and expenses are booked. KAMs belong to the Key Accounts cost centre; customer-specific expenses are tagged with the customer code. Your cost centre is shown in the HR portal. Source: Organisation FAQ §10 Finance.

### Q: Who maintains customer master data?
Finance creates and maintains customer codes, billing addresses, GSTIN and credit limits after a KYC check; the KAM requests changes with supporting documents. Source: Organisation FAQ §10 Finance.

### Q: Who decides plant allocation for a new part?
Operations and SCM decide the producing plant during the RFQ feasibility based on capacity, process capability and logistics; the KAM communicates the decision and its delivery implications to the customer. Source: Organisation FAQ §9 Plants.

### Q: Who maintains the CRM?
Key Accounts operations (CRM administrator) maintains the CRM configuration and reports; every KAM keeps their accounts, contacts, visits, RFQs and opportunities current. Source: Organisation FAQ §13 IT.

### Q: Who do I contact about the organisation chart?
Your HRBP for the organisation chart; your Mentor for who to work with on your account. Source: Organisation FAQ §23 Contacts.
