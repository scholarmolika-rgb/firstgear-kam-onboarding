# Policy FAQ — Leave Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Entitlements and rules in this document are illustrative model policies for training — confirm current balances and rules in the HR portal or with your HRBP. Statutory leave (for example maternity) follows applicable Indian law.

Key rules at a glance (leave year January–December): Earned Leave (EL) 18 days a year, credited 1.5 days a month, carry-forward cap 45 days. Casual Leave (CL) 8 days, no carry-forward. Sick Leave (SL) 7 days, carry-forward up to 21 days. Maternity 26 weeks (for the first two children). Paternity 5 working days. Bereavement 3 days. Marriage 3 days. Holidays: 10 declared plus 2 optional.

### Q: What types of leave are available?
Earned Leave (EL), Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), maternity, adoption, paternity, bereavement and marriage leave, compensatory off, optional holidays and leave without pay (LOP). Each type has its own entitlement and rules, described in this FAQ. Balances are shown in the HR portal under "My leave". Source: Leave Policy FAQ §1 Leave types.

### Q: When does the leave year start?
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Balances, carry-forward and lapse are calculated at the end of December. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §1 Leave year.

### Q: How much Earned Leave do I get?
18 days a year, credited at 1.5 days at the start of each month. If you join mid-month, the credit for that month is pro-rated. EL is meant for planned vacations and personal needs. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §2 Earned Leave.

### Q: When can I start using Earned Leave as a new joiner?
EL accrues from your joining date but can be availed after you complete three months of service, except in emergencies approved by your Reporting Boss and HR. During the 30-day onboarding journey, planned EL is not normally approved because gates, sessions and assessments are scheduled. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §2 Earned Leave.

### Q: How much Earned Leave can I carry forward?
Unused EL is carried forward up to a total balance of 45 days. Anything above 45 at 31 December lapses. Plan vacations during the year rather than accumulating. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §2 Carry-forward.

### Q: Can I encash Earned Leave?
EL is encashed only at separation, up to the 45-day cap, at basic salary per day (monthly basic ÷ 30). There is no annual encashment. Encashment is paid with the full and final settlement. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §2 Encashment.

### Q: How much Casual Leave do I get?
8 days a year, credited in full on 1 January (pro-rated if you join during the year). CL is for short, unplanned personal needs. Unused CL lapses on 31 December. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §3 Casual Leave.

### Q: How many days of Casual Leave can I take at a time?
A maximum of 3 consecutive days. CL cannot be combined with EL; it can be combined with SL only with HR approval. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §3 Casual Leave.

### Q: How much Sick Leave do I get?
7 days a year, credited on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners). Unused SL is carried forward up to a total of 21 days. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §4 Sick Leave.

### Q: Do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?
Yes, for more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave, upload a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner in the HR portal within 3 days of returning. For more than 7 days, a fitness certificate is also needed before rejoining. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §4 Sick Leave.

### Q: What if I fall sick and have no sick leave left?
Unused CL and then EL can be used for illness. If no balance is left, the absence is treated as leave without pay. For serious illness or hospitalisation, speak to your HRBP — special medical leave may be considered case by case. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §4 Sick Leave.

### Q: How do I apply for leave?
Apply in the HR portal under "My leave": choose the type, dates and reason; the request goes to your Reporting Boss for approval. For unplanned leave (illness, emergency) inform your Reporting Boss by phone or message before 10:00 on the day and apply in the portal within 2 working days of returning. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §5 Applying.

### Q: How far in advance should I apply for planned leave?
At least 7 days in advance for EL of 3 days or more, and 3 days in advance for shorter planned leave. Long vacations (more than 7 days) should be discussed with your Reporting Boss at least 30 days ahead so customer cover can be arranged. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §5 Applying.

### Q: What must a KAM arrange before going on leave?
Arrange cover for your accounts: brief the covering KAM or your Reporting Boss on open RFQs, deliveries at risk, open 8Ds and commitments due during your absence; set an out-of-office reply naming the cover; tell key customer contacts in advance for leave longer than 3 days; and make sure no quotation deadline or customer review falls unattended. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §6 Account cover.

### Q: Can my leave be rejected?
Yes. Your Reporting Boss may decline or reschedule planned leave for business reasons — for example a customer audit, launch, quarterly review or quotation deadline. The reason is recorded in the portal; HR can help resolve disagreements. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §5 Approval.

### Q: Are weekends and holidays counted inside a leave period?
For EL of 5 days or more, weekly offs and holidays falling inside the leave period are counted as leave ("sandwich" rule). For CL, SL and EL of less than 5 days, they are not counted. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §7 Counting.

### Q: Can I take half-day leave?
Yes. Half-day CL or EL can be applied in the HR portal (first or second half). Half-day SL is not available. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §7 Half days.

### Q: How much maternity leave is available?
26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. For a third or later child, 12 weeks apply. After maternity leave, work-from-home may be agreed with your Reporting Boss where the role allows. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §8 Maternity.

### Q: Is there leave for adoption?
A mother adopting a child below three months of age, or a commissioning mother, receives 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Fathers may take paternity leave on adoption. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §8 Adoption.

### Q: How much paternity leave is available?
5 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption, in one block or two. Apply in the HR portal with the birth certificate or adoption papers. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §8 Paternity.

### Q: Is there leave for bereavement?
Yes. 3 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of a spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law. Inform your Reporting Boss; apply in the portal on return. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §9 Bereavement.

### Q: Is there leave for my own wedding?
Yes. 3 days of paid marriage leave, once during your employment, which can be combined with EL. Apply at least 30 days in advance. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §9 Marriage.

### Q: What is a compensatory off and how do I claim it?
If you work on a weekly off or declared holiday at the company's request — for example a customer audit, launch support or a weekend plant trial — you can claim one compensatory off per full day worked (half a day for 4 or more hours). Get your Reporting Boss's approval before working, record the day in the HR portal, and take the comp-off within 60 days, after which it lapses. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §10 Compensatory off.

### Q: Does travel on a Sunday give me a compensatory off?
Travel alone on a weekly off does not earn a comp-off. If you travel and also attend a customer meeting or work at a plant that day, a comp-off can be claimed with your Reporting Boss's approval. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §10 Compensatory off.

### Q: How do optional holidays work?
Choose any 2 holidays from the optional list published each December and apply for them in the HR portal like leave. Unused optional holidays lapse at year-end. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §11 Holidays.

### Q: What happens if a holiday falls during my approved leave?
A declared holiday inside CL, SL or EL of less than 5 days is not counted as leave. For EL of 5 days or more, the sandwich rule applies. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §11 Holidays.

### Q: What is leave without pay?
If you have no leave balance, approved absence is recorded as leave without pay (LOP), and salary is reduced pro-rata (monthly gross ÷ days in month for each day). LOP of more than 30 days in a year needs Head of HR approval and affects EL accrual for that period. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §12 Leave without pay.

### Q: Can I take leave during my notice period?
EL during the notice period is normally not approved, because it is needed for handover; balances are encashed instead. SL with a medical certificate is allowed. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §13 Notice period.

### Q: Can I cancel or change approved leave?
Yes. Cancel or modify it in the HR portal before the start date; your Reporting Boss is notified. If you return early, cancel the unused days so the balance is restored. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §5 Changes.

### Q: What if I am called back from leave for a customer emergency?
If your Reporting Boss recalls you from approved leave, the unused days are restored to your balance and reasonable extra travel costs to return are reimbursed. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §6 Recall.

### Q: Where can I see my leave balance?
In the HR portal under "My leave", which shows EL, CL, SL, comp-off and optional-holiday balances, pending requests and history. Your monthly payslip also shows the EL balance. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §14 Records.

### Q: What if my leave balance looks wrong?
Raise an HR helpdesk ticket under "Leave" with the dates in question. Corrections are made within 5 working days. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §14 Records.

### Q: Is leave during probation different?
CL and SL are available pro-rata from joining. EL accrues but is usable only after 3 months, except emergencies approved by HR. Absences during the 30-day onboarding may delay your gates, because the journey follows evidence, not dates. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §15 Probation.

### Q: Does leave affect my onboarding journey?
The journey is not paused automatically. Tasks keep their due dates and may become overdue. If you need leave during the first 30 days, agree it with your Reporting Boss and Mentor in advance; for longer absences HR can reset the start date of your onboarding. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §15 Onboarding.

### Q: Can I combine leave with an official trip?
Yes, with your Reporting Boss's approval, provided there is no extra cost to the company. Hotel and per diem stop on the last business day; travel dates must reflect the business purpose. See the Travel Policy FAQ. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §16 Leave with travel.

### Q: Is there special leave for voting, jury or exams?
Voting is a paid holiday where the law requires it for your location. Leave to appear in exams for company-approved education is granted as EL; up to 3 days of special leave a year can be approved by HR for approved certifications. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §17 Special leave.

### Q: What leave applies to plant shift employees?
Plant employees follow the plant leave rules under the Factories Act and applicable state rules, which may differ from this headquarters policy. If you are posted at a plant, check with the plant HR. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §18 Plants.

### Q: What happens to leave balances on transfer?
All balances move with you. If the new location follows different holiday lists, the new list applies from your transfer date. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §19 Transfers.

### Q: What happens to my leave on resignation?
CL and SL lapse. EL up to 45 days is encashed at basic salary per day in the full and final settlement. Negative EL (leave taken in advance) is recovered. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §13 Exit.

### Q: Who do I contact about the Leave Policy?
Your Reporting Boss for approvals; your HRBP or the HR helpdesk (category "Leave") for balances and rules. Source: Leave Policy FAQ §20 Contacts.
