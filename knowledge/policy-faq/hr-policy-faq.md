# Policy FAQ — HR Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. FirstGear's company profile uses publicly reported data; internal HR rules, grades, amounts and timelines in this document are illustrative model policies for training — always confirm the current position with your HR Business Partner (HRBP) before acting. Statutory benefits follow applicable Indian law.

Key rules at a glance: grades G1 (Executive/Senior Executive), G2 (Assistant Manager/Manager — most KAMs), G3 (Senior Manager/Senior KAM), G4 (AGM/DGM, e.g. Head of Key Accounts), G5 (GM and above). Probation 6 months. Notice period 30 days in probation, 60 days for confirmed G1–G2, 90 days for G3 and above. Office hours 9:00–17:30, Monday–Friday, at headquarters.

### Q: Which grade is a Key Account Manager in?
Most KAMs are in grade G2 (Assistant Manager/Manager); Senior KAMs, who often act as Mentors, are G3; the Head of Key Accounts (the usual Reporting Boss) is G4. Your grade is printed in your appointment letter and drives your travel, hotel and reimbursement entitlements, notice period and approval levels. If your grade is not visible in the HR portal, raise a ticket with your HRBP. Source: HR Policy FAQ §1 Grades.

### Q: What documents must I submit on joining?
Signed appointment letter, identity proof (Aadhaar or passport), PAN card, address proof, educational and experience certificates, relieving letter from the previous employer, last three salary slips, bank account details for salary, two photographs, emergency contact details, nominations for PF, gratuity and insurance, and the signed Code of Conduct acknowledgement. Upload them in the HR portal within your first week; originals are verified once by HR. Source: HR Policy FAQ §2 Joining.

### Q: Is there a background verification?
Yes. Employment history, education, address and criminal-record checks are done by an approved agency after you join. Discrepancies are discussed with you first; deliberate misrepresentation can lead to termination. You will receive a consent form in the HR portal — sign it in the first week so verification is not delayed. Source: HR Policy FAQ §2 Joining.

### Q: How long is probation and how is confirmation decided?
Probation is 6 months from the joining date. Your Reporting Boss assesses performance, conduct and — for KAMs — the outcome of the 30-day readiness journey (the Day-30 sign-off). HR issues a confirmation letter at the end of month 6. Probation can be extended once by up to 3 months with written reasons. Source: HR Policy FAQ §3 Probation.

### Q: Does the 30-day onboarding decide my confirmation?
It is an important input but not the only one. A READY sign-off at Day 30 is strong evidence for confirmation; an EXTENDED decision normally means probation continues while you complete the agreed development actions. Confirmation is always a human decision by your Reporting Boss with HR, at month 6. Source: HR Policy FAQ §3 Probation.

### Q: What are the office working hours?
Headquarters works Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30, with a 45-minute lunch break. Core hours are 10:00–16:00; you may start between 8:30 and 10:00 provided you complete 8 hours. Plants work six days a week in shifts — when you are at a plant, follow plant timings and safety rules. Source: HR Policy FAQ §4 Working hours.

### Q: How is attendance recorded for a KAM who is often in the field?
At the office, attendance is recorded by the access card. On customer visits or travel, mark "On Duty (OD)" in the HR portal with the customer name and purpose by the next working day; your Reporting Boss approves it. Unmarked days are treated as absence after 5 working days. Source: HR Policy FAQ §4 Attendance.

### Q: Can I work from home?
G2 and above may work from home up to 4 days a month with prior approval from the Reporting Boss in the HR portal, provided no customer, plant or review commitment is affected. Work from home is not available during the first 30 days of onboarding, because the journey relies on sessions, plant walks and mentor time. Source: HR Policy FAQ §4 Flexible working.

### Q: What is the dress code?
Business casual at the office; business formal for customer meetings, reviews and audits. Safety shoes and the PPE issued at the gate are mandatory on any shop floor, whatever you are wearing. Wear your ID card visibly at all times. Source: HR Policy FAQ §5 Dress code.

### Q: When is salary paid?
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month to your registered bank account. Payslips are published in the HR portal on the same day. Joining in the middle of a month gives a pro-rata salary for the days worked. Report payroll errors to the payroll helpdesk within 7 days. Source: HR Policy FAQ §6 Payroll.

### Q: What does my CTC include?
Cost to company typically includes basic salary, house rent allowance, special allowance, the employer's provident fund contribution, gratuity provision, insurance premiums and target variable pay. Your exact break-up is in your appointment letter annexure and in the HR portal under "My compensation". Source: HR Policy FAQ §6 Compensation.

### Q: How does provident fund work?
You and the company each contribute 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund, as required by the EPF Act. Your Universal Account Number (UAN) appears on your payslip; link it with Aadhaar and your bank account on the EPFO portal. If you had a previous UAN, give it to HR so your balance can be transferred. Source: HR Policy FAQ §6 Statutory benefits.

### Q: Am I eligible for gratuity?
Gratuity is paid as required by the Payment of Gratuity Act (and the labour codes as they apply) — typically after five years of continuous service, at 15 days of last-drawn basic salary for each completed year, subject to the statutory ceiling. Nominate your beneficiary in the HR portal during your first month. Source: HR Policy FAQ §6 Statutory benefits.

### Q: What insurance cover do I have?
Group mediclaim of ₹5 lakh per year on a family floater basis (you, your spouse and up to two children), cashless at network hospitals; parents can be added at your own cost during the annual enrolment window. You are also covered by group personal accident insurance (three times annual CTC) and group term life insurance. The e-card is in the HR portal. Source: HR Policy FAQ §7 Insurance.

### Q: How do I add my spouse or newborn to the medical policy?
Add them in the HR portal within 30 days of marriage or birth with the marriage or birth certificate. After 30 days, dependants can only be added at the next annual enrolment. Source: HR Policy FAQ §7 Insurance.

### Q: How do I submit my income-tax declaration?
Declare planned investments and your tax regime choice in the HR portal in April; payroll deducts tax (TDS) accordingly. Upload proofs (rent receipts, insurance, investments, home-loan certificate) by 15 January; anything not proved is taxed in the last three months. Form 16 is issued by 15 June. Source: HR Policy FAQ §6 Taxation.

### Q: How does the performance appraisal cycle work?
The performance year runs April to March. Goals (for KAMs, the KPI scorecard) are set in April, reviewed mid-year in October and assessed in April. Ratings run from 1 (below expectations) to 5 (outstanding) and drive salary revision, variable pay and promotion eligibility. See the KPI Policy FAQ for KAM measures. Source: HR Policy FAQ §8 Performance.

### Q: When is my first appraisal if I join mid-year?
If you join before 1 October you are appraised in the April cycle on a pro-rata basis; if you join on or after 1 October, your first full appraisal is in the following April and any salary revision is at HR's discretion. Source: HR Policy FAQ §8 Performance.

### Q: How do promotions work?
Promotions are considered once a year after the appraisal. Normally you need at least two years in your grade, ratings of 4 or 5 in the last two cycles, and a role at the next level. For KAMs, handling a larger or strategic account is the usual route. Your Reporting Boss nominates; a committee chaired by the function head decides. Source: HR Policy FAQ §8 Promotions.

### Q: How is a KAM's variable pay calculated?
Variable pay is a percentage of CTC set by grade and paid annually after the appraisal. The payout factor follows your rating (rating 5: 150%, 4: 120%, 3: 100%, 2: 50%, 1: nil). A proven Code of Conduct breach removes variable pay for that year. Details are in the KPI Policy FAQ. Source: HR Policy FAQ §8 Variable pay.

### Q: How many training hours am I expected to complete?
Every employee should complete at least 40 hours of learning a year, including the mandatory e-modules (Code of Conduct, prevention of sexual harassment, insider trading, competition law and health and safety), which must be passed every year. The 30-day onboarding counts towards your first year. Source: HR Policy FAQ §9 Learning.

### Q: Can the company transfer me to another location or account?
Yes. Your appointment letter allows transfer to any location, plant or account based on business needs. Transfers are discussed with you in advance; relocation support (travel for the family, shifting of household goods, 15 days of temporary accommodation) is provided for company-initiated transfers. A KAM moving to a new account goes through a shortened onboarding as a "reassigned KAM". Source: HR Policy FAQ §10 Transfers.

### Q: What is the employee referral scheme?
Refer candidates through the HR portal. If a referred candidate joins and completes probation, you receive a referral bonus of ₹25,000 (₹40,000 for G3 and above roles). HR team members and the hiring manager for that role are not eligible. Source: HR Policy FAQ §11 Referrals.

### Q: Can I take up part-time work or a side business?
No, not without written approval from the Head of HR. Any outside work, directorship or business interest must be declared in the annual conflict-of-interest declaration, and anything involving a customer, supplier or competitor is not allowed. Source: HR Policy FAQ §12 Outside employment.

### Q: How do I raise a workplace grievance?
Raise it first with your Reporting Boss or HRBP. If unresolved within 7 working days, escalate to the Head of HR, who responds within 15 working days. You can also use the confidential ethics line. Grievances are handled without retaliation. Sexual-harassment complaints go to the Internal Committee instead. Source: HR Policy FAQ §13 Grievances.

### Q: What is the policy on prevention of sexual harassment (POSH)?
FirstGear follows the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC), in writing, normally within three months of the incident. The IC completes its inquiry within 90 days, keeps the matter confidential and protects the complainant against retaliation. IC members are listed on the intranet. Source: HR Policy FAQ §13 POSH.

### Q: Does POSH apply at customer sites or events?
Yes. The workplace includes customer premises, plants, travel, hotels, conferences and company events. If you face or witness inappropriate behaviour from a customer employee, inform the IC and your Reporting Boss; the company will take it up with the customer. Source: HR Policy FAQ §13 POSH.

### Q: Is there an employee assistance programme?
Yes. A confidential counselling helpline is available 24×7 for you and your family, for stress, health or personal concerns. Up to six free counselling sessions a year are covered. The number is in the HR portal; the company does not see who uses it. Source: HR Policy FAQ §14 Wellbeing.

### Q: What happens if I get married or have a child?
Update your marital status and dependants in the HR portal within 30 days, add them to mediclaim, and apply for marriage, maternity or paternity leave as per the Leave Policy. Update nominations for PF, gratuity and insurance. Source: HR Policy FAQ §7 Life events.

### Q: How do I update my personal details or bank account?
Update address, phone and emergency contact in the HR portal yourself. Bank-account changes need a cancelled cheque or bank letter and take effect from the next payroll if submitted before the 20th. Name changes need supporting documents verified by HR. Source: HR Policy FAQ §15 Employee records.

### Q: Can I get an employment or salary certificate?
Yes. Request it in the HR portal under "Letters"; standard letters (employment, address, salary for visa or loan) are issued digitally within 3 working days. Source: HR Policy FAQ §15 Employee records.

### Q: What is the notice period if I resign?
30 days during probation; 60 days for confirmed G1 and G2; 90 days for G3 and above. Resign through the HR portal so the date is recorded. Notice can be shortened by mutual agreement with your Reporting Boss; any shortfall may be recovered from your final settlement. Source: HR Policy FAQ §16 Exit.

### Q: What must a KAM hand over before leaving?
A written account handover to the incoming KAM or your Reporting Boss: the account plan, open RFQs and quotations, open commitments and 8Ds, stakeholder map, pending claims and collections, CRM records up to date, and a joint introduction meeting with the customer where possible. Return the laptop, mobile, ID card and any customer documents. Source: HR Policy FAQ §16 Exit.

### Q: When is the full and final settlement paid?
Within 45 days of your last working day, after clearance from IT, Admin, Finance and your function. It includes salary for days worked, leave encashment as per the Leave Policy, and statutory dues, less recoveries. Relieving and experience letters are issued with it. Source: HR Policy FAQ §16 Exit.

### Q: Can I re-join FirstGear later?
Yes. Former employees with a good record may re-apply after six months. Service is not normally continued for benefits unless HR agrees otherwise in writing. Source: HR Policy FAQ §16 Re-hire.

### Q: What are the company holidays?
Ten declared holidays plus two optional holidays from a published list each calendar year. The list is published in December for the following year and differs slightly by location. Plants may have different holidays because of production schedules. Source: HR Policy FAQ §17 Holidays.

### Q: What is the policy on overtime for managers?
Managers (G2 and above) are not paid overtime. If you work on a weekly off or holiday at the company's request, you may claim a compensatory off as described in the Leave Policy. Source: HR Policy FAQ §4 Overtime.

### Q: Who is my HR Business Partner and what do they do?
Each function has an HRBP who handles your HR queries, onboarding paperwork, performance and career conversations, transfers and exits. Your HRBP's name is on your profile page in the HR portal; for routine questions (payslip, letters, insurance) use the HR helpdesk ticket first. Source: HR Policy FAQ §18 HR contacts.

### Q: How do I raise an HR helpdesk ticket?
In the HR portal choose "Help", pick the category (payroll, leave, insurance, letters, onboarding, other) and describe the issue. Standard tickets are answered within 2 working days; payroll tickets raised before the 20th are fixed in that month's payroll. Source: HR Policy FAQ §18 HR contacts.

### Q: What happens if I am absent without informing anyone?
Inform your Reporting Boss as early as possible on the day. Absence without information for three consecutive working days triggers a check by HR; unexplained absence beyond eight working days is treated as unauthorised and may lead to disciplinary action. Source: HR Policy FAQ §19 Discipline.

### Q: What is the disciplinary process?
For misconduct, HR issues a written show-cause notice; you reply within 7 days. Depending on severity, outcomes range from a written warning to termination after an inquiry. Serious breaches — bribery, fraud, harassment, safety violations, leaking customer data — may lead to suspension pending inquiry. Source: HR Policy FAQ §19 Discipline.

### Q: Does the company support higher education?
Yes. Employees with at least two years of service and a rating of 3 or above can apply for education support of up to 50% of fees (maximum ₹1.5 lakh) for a relevant programme, approved by the function head and HR. A two-year service commitment applies after completion. Source: HR Policy FAQ §9 Education support.

### Q: What is the policy on diversity and equal opportunity?
FirstGear is an equal-opportunity employer. Decisions on hiring, pay, promotion and assignments are based on merit, without discrimination on gender, religion, caste, disability, age or any other protected characteristic. Concerns can be raised with HR or the ethics line. Source: HR Policy FAQ §20 Diversity.

### Q: Can I bring a family member to a company event or the plant?
Family days and events are announced by HR. Family members may not enter a plant or restricted area except on an organised family day with a visitor pass and safety briefing. Source: HR Policy FAQ §21 Events.

### Q: Where are HR policies published?
The complete HR policy manual is in the HR portal under "Policies", with the version and effective date. The Compass knowledge base holds these FAQs for quick answers; if they ever differ, the published policy in the HR portal applies. Source: HR Policy FAQ §22 Policy publication.

### Q: Who do I contact about the HR Policy?
Your HRBP or the HR helpdesk in the HR portal. For confidential matters, contact the Head of HR directly or use the ethics line. Source: HR Policy FAQ §18 HR contacts.
