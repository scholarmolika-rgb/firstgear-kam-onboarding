# Policy FAQ — Local Conveyance Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Rates and limits here are illustrative model policies for training — confirm current values with Finance before claiming.

Key rules at a glance: local conveyance covers official travel within your base city or within 100 km of it. Own car ₹12 per km, own two-wheeler ₹5 per km, app cabs and taxis at actuals with e-receipts (economy category), autos and public transport up to ₹300 a day without receipts on self-declaration, tolls and parking at actuals. Every claim must match a visit logged in the CRM or an approved internal trip. Claim monthly, within 30 days.

### Q: What is local conveyance?
Travel for official work within your base city or within 100 km of it — customer meetings, plant visits, supplier visits, bank or government offices on company work — that does not involve an overnight stay. Travel beyond 100 km or with a night away is covered by the Travel and Conveyance Policy. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: Is my daily commute to the office reimbursed?
No. Travel between home and your normal place of work is your own cost. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §2 Commute.

### Q: What if I go directly from home to a customer?
You can claim the distance from home to the customer and back, minus your normal one-way commute distance to the office on that day. If the customer is on your way to the office, there is no claim. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §2 Home to customer.

### Q: What rate do I get for using my own car?
₹12 per km, covering fuel, maintenance and depreciation, plus tolls and parking at actuals with receipts. The vehicle must be insured, and you must hold a valid licence. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §3 Own vehicle.

### Q: What rate do I get for using my own two-wheeler?
₹5 per km, plus parking at actuals. Wear a helmet; two-wheelers should not be used to carry samples or for trips longer than 50 km one way. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §3 Own vehicle.

### Q: How do I record kilometres?
Log each trip in the CRM visit record or the expense claim with the date, from and to locations, purpose and customer, and the kilometres from a maps route (the claim tool calculates the route distance). Odometer photos may be requested for long trips. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §4 Recording.

### Q: Is there a monthly kilometre limit?
Own-vehicle claims up to 1,500 km per month (car) or 1,000 km (two-wheeler) need only the normal approval. Above that, your Reporting Boss must confirm the business need in the claim; consistently high mileage is reviewed quarterly by Finance. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §5 Limits.

### Q: Can I use app cabs?
Yes. App cabs (economy or sedan category) are reimbursed at actuals with the e-receipt showing date, route and fare. Premium or SUV categories are allowed only when carrying samples or travelling with customers. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §6 Cabs.

### Q: Do I need receipts for autos, buses or the metro?
For autos, buses and metro, up to ₹300 a day can be claimed on a self-declaration without receipts. Above that, attach receipts or the metro card statement. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §6 Public transport.

### Q: Are tolls and parking reimbursed?
Yes, at actuals with receipts or the FASTag statement showing the transactions. Parking fines or towing charges are not reimbursed. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §7 Tolls and parking.

### Q: Can I claim conveyance during the 30-day onboarding?
Yes, for official trips: plant walks, sessions at other locations and, from Phase 2, customer visits you attend with your Mentor or Reporting Boss. Link each trip to the session or visit in the claim. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §8 Onboarding.

### Q: Is there a pool car I can use instead?
Yes. Book a pool car with driver through the Admin helpdesk at least 24 hours ahead for plant or customer visits; it is preferred for plant visits and when several colleagues travel together. No conveyance is claimed for pool-car trips. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §9 Pool cars.

### Q: What if I work late?
If you leave the office after 21:00 on official work, an app cab home is reimbursed. For safety, the company arranges or reimburses a cab home for any employee leaving after 22:00, and women employees may request an escorted cab through Admin after 20:00. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §10 Late working.

### Q: Can I claim travel to the airport or station for a business trip?
That is part of the business trip and is claimed under the Travel Policy, not local conveyance. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: Can two employees claim for the same car trip?
No. Only the driver or vehicle owner claims the kilometres; passengers claim nothing for that trip. Car-pooling to the same customer is encouraged. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §11 Shared trips.

### Q: When must I submit local conveyance claims?
Monthly, in the expense module, within 30 days of the month end. Claims older than 60 days are not paid unless the CFO approves. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §12 Claims.

### Q: Who approves local conveyance claims?
Your Reporting Boss approves, then Finance (Accounts Payable) checks and pays to your salary account within 10 working days. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §12 Approvals.

### Q: What if the customer meeting is cancelled after I set out?
The trip is still claimable — note "meeting cancelled by customer" in the CRM visit and the claim. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §4 Recording.

### Q: Can I claim if I combine an official visit with personal errands?
Only the official portion — the direct route between official locations. Personal detours are not claimable. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §11 Personal use.

### Q: Is fuel for a company pool car reimbursed to me?
No. Pool cars are fuelled by Admin through fuel cards. If you are asked to refuel in an emergency, claim it with the bill and the vehicle number. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §9 Pool cars.

### Q: Do I need to keep my vehicle insured?
Yes. Comprehensive insurance and a valid PUC certificate are your responsibility when you use your own vehicle on company work. The company is not liable for damage to your vehicle; accidents must be reported to your Reporting Boss and EHS. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §13 Insurance and safety.

### Q: What are the safe driving rules on company work?
No phone use while driving (hands-free only for urgent calls), seat belts for everyone, helmet on two-wheelers, no driving under the influence, no driving more than 4 hours without a break, and avoid self-driving after 22:00. Report incidents and near misses to EHS. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §13 Insurance and safety.

### Q: Is there a monthly conveyance allowance instead of claims?
No. Conveyance is reimbursed only against official trips. Some field roles have a fixed vehicle allowance in their salary structure; if yours does, you claim tolls and parking only, not kilometres. Check your compensation annexure. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §14 Allowances.

### Q: How does the CRM link to my conveyance claim?
Each customer trip should have a CRM visit record (customer, date, contacts met, purpose and outcome). The expense tool matches claim lines to visits; unmatched lines are queried by Finance. Keeping the CRM current saves time on approval. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §4 Recording.

### Q: Can I claim travel to a supplier or sub-contractor?
Yes, if it is part of your role (for example resolving a delivery issue with SCM). Note the purpose and who asked you to attend. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: Is parking at headquarters reimbursed?
No. Office parking is free for registered vehicles. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §7 Tolls and parking.

### Q: What if I use a rented self-drive car?
Self-drive rentals need prior approval from your Reporting Boss and must be booked through the travel desk so insurance is covered. Fuel is reimbursed at actuals with bills; the per-km rate does not apply. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §6 Rentals.

### Q: How are rates revised?
Finance reviews per-km rates once a year (April) based on fuel prices, and may revise them mid-year if fuel prices change by more than 10%. The rate on the date of the trip applies. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §15 Rate revision.

### Q: Can I claim a cab when carrying samples or documents?
Yes. When carrying samples, prototypes or bulky material for a customer, an app cab or a hired car is reimbursed at actuals; note the delivery challan number in the claim. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §6 Cabs.

### Q: What if my claim is rejected?
Finance or your Reporting Boss records the reason in the expense tool. Correct and resubmit within 15 days, for example by adding a missing receipt or the CRM visit. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §12 Rejections.

### Q: Can I claim the cost of travel between two of our offices or plants?
Yes, when you are asked to attend a meeting, review or training at another company location. Use the company bus or pool car where available. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: Is a driver's allowance paid if I hire a car with a driver?
For hired cars booked through the travel desk, the vendor bills the driver's charges; you do not pay separately. For cars you hire yourself in an emergency, the driver's allowance on the bill is reimbursed. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §6 Rentals.

### Q: Are e-bikes or bicycles covered?
Bicycles are not reimbursed per km. Electric two-wheelers are treated like other two-wheelers at ₹5 per km. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §3 Own vehicle.

### Q: What records should I keep?
Receipts for cabs, tolls and parking (digital copies are fine), the CRM visit record, and your mileage log. Keep them for 12 months in case of an audit. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §16 Records.

### Q: Who do I contact about the Local Conveyance Policy?
Finance (Accounts Payable) through the expense module helpdesk, or your Reporting Boss. Source: Local Conveyance Policy FAQ §17 Contacts.
