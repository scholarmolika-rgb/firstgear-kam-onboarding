# Policy FAQ — IT and Information Security Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Technical settings and timelines here are illustrative model policies for training — follow the IT helpdesk's current instructions where they differ.

Key rules at a glance: use only company systems and approved tools for company data. Passwords of at least 12 characters with multi-factor authentication (MFA). Data classes — Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted; customer drawings and prices are Restricted. Report a lost device within 2 hours and a suspected phishing email immediately. Never enter customer or company confidential data into public AI tools. USB storage is blocked by default.

### Q: Which systems will a KAM use?
Email and calendar, the CRM (accounts, contacts, visits, RFQs, opportunities), the ERP (orders, prices, invoices, receivables — read access), the document management system, the employee portal (HR, expenses, travel, Admin and IT helpdesks), the Compass onboarding app, customer portals and the approved file-sharing service. Source: IT Policy FAQ §1 Systems.

### Q: How do I get system access?
IT creates your email and core access before Day 1. CRM, ERP and shared-folder access are requested in the IT helpdesk and approved by your Reporting Boss; restricted folders need the data owner's approval. Access is reviewed quarterly and removed when you change role or leave. Source: IT Policy FAQ §2 Access.

### Q: What are the password rules?
At least 12 characters, not reused from other sites, changed every 90 days, and protected by multi-factor authentication on email, VPN and the CRM. Never share your password — IT will never ask for it. Use the company password manager for system passwords. Source: IT Policy FAQ §3 Passwords.

### Q: What is multi-factor authentication and why is it required?
A second check (an authenticator-app code or approval) on top of your password. It stops most account takeovers even if a password is stolen. Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start — report it to IT Security immediately. Source: IT Policy FAQ §3 MFA.

### Q: How is company information classified?
Public (published, for example the corporate presentation), Internal (general company information), Confidential (business plans, internal reports, employee data) and Restricted (customer drawings and specifications, prices and cost models, unpublished financial results). Restricted data is shared only on a need-to-know basis through approved, encrypted channels. Source: IT Policy FAQ §4 Classification.

### Q: How should I share files with a customer?
Use the approved file-sharing service or the customer's portal, with access limited to named people and an expiry date. Do not email large drawing packages or send them through personal email, personal cloud storage or messaging apps. Source: IT Policy FAQ §5 File sharing.

### Q: Can I use WhatsApp or other messaging apps with customers?
For quick coordination only (for example meeting logistics). Never share drawings, prices, quotations, quality data or commitments on messaging apps; send them through email or approved channels so there is a record. Source: IT Policy FAQ §5 Messaging.

### Q: Can I forward work email to my personal email?
No. Automatic or manual forwarding of company email to personal accounts is not allowed and is blocked for Restricted content. Use the company mobile apps for email on the go. Source: IT Policy FAQ §6 Email.

### Q: How do I recognise a phishing email?
Unexpected urgency, payment or bank-detail change requests, links to login pages, attachments you did not expect, mismatched sender domains, or requests to bypass approval. Customers' "new bank details" are a common fraud — Finance verifies any bank-detail change by phone through known contacts. Source: IT Policy FAQ §7 Phishing.

### Q: What do I do if I clicked a suspicious link?
Disconnect from the network if possible, report immediately to IT Security through the helpdesk "Report a security incident" button or phone, and change your password if you entered it. Fast reporting limits damage; there is no blame for honest reporting. Source: IT Policy FAQ §7 Incident reporting.

### Q: What if I lose my laptop or phone, or it is stolen?
Report it to IT Security within 2 hours so it can be locked and wiped remotely, then to Admin, and file a police report within 24 hours. A lost device with customer data is a security incident that may need to be reported to the customer. Source: IT Policy FAQ §8 Lost devices.

### Q: When must I use the VPN?
Whenever you access internal systems from outside the office, and always on public or hotel Wi-Fi. Avoid unknown public Wi-Fi for sensitive work; use your company mobile hotspot instead. Source: IT Policy FAQ §9 Remote access.

### Q: Can I use a USB drive?
USB storage is blocked by default. If you need to exchange files with a customer at their site, use the approved file-sharing service; exceptions for encrypted company USB drives are approved by IT Security for a fixed period. Source: IT Policy FAQ §10 Removable media.

### Q: Can I install software on my laptop?
Only from the company software centre. Other software needs an IT helpdesk request with your Reporting Boss's approval; IT checks licensing and security. Pirated or unlicensed software is never allowed. Source: IT Policy FAQ §11 Software.

### Q: Can I use public AI tools such as chatbots for work?
Only for non-confidential tasks (for example improving the language of a public text). Never paste customer names with prices, drawings, cost data, quotations, contracts or employee data into public AI tools. Use the company-approved assistants — Ask FirstGear in the Compass answers from approved documents. Source: IT Policy FAQ §12 AI tools.

### Q: What is the policy on social media?
Do not post about customers, programmes, prices, plant images or unpublished company information. You may share the company's official posts. If you identify yourself as a FirstGear employee, make clear opinions are your own. Media or analyst contacts go to Corporate Communications. Source: IT Policy FAQ §13 Social media.

### Q: Can I use my personal phone for work email?
Yes, through the company mobile-device management app, which separates work data and lets IT wipe only the work profile if the phone is lost. Do not save customer attachments to the personal side of the phone. Source: IT Policy FAQ §14 Personal devices.

### Q: How long is email and CRM data kept?
Email is retained for 7 years; CRM records for the life of the customer relationship plus 8 years; deleting records to avoid retention is not allowed. Legal may place a hold on records for disputes or audits. Source: IT Policy FAQ §15 Retention.

### Q: Is my use of company systems monitored?
Company systems are monitored for security, as stated in the acceptable-use policy you accept at joining. Monitoring follows applicable law and is not used to read personal content except in a formal investigation authorised by Legal and HR. Source: IT Policy FAQ §16 Monitoring.

### Q: How is personal data of customers' employees protected?
Contact details of customer employees are personal data. Keep them only in the CRM, use them only for the business relationship, do not export lists to personal files, and follow India's data-protection law and the customer's requirements. Source: IT Policy FAQ §17 Personal data.

### Q: Can I record a customer meeting?
Only with the consent of all participants, announced at the start, and only on company systems. Recordings are Confidential and kept no longer than needed. Source: IT Policy FAQ §18 Recordings.

### Q: What should I do before travelling abroad with my laptop?
Make sure it is encrypted and patched, carry only the customer data you need, always use the VPN, and never leave the laptop unattended. Some countries require special care — check with IT Security for travel to high-risk destinations. Source: IT Policy FAQ §19 Travel.

### Q: How do I print confidential documents?
Use secure "pull" printing (release at the printer with your ID card) and collect prints immediately. Shred Restricted printouts in the locked bins. Avoid printing drawings unless necessary. Source: IT Policy FAQ §20 Printing.

### Q: How do I report an IT problem?
Raise a ticket in the IT helpdesk in the employee portal (or call the helpdesk number for urgent issues such as no email before a customer meeting). Priority-1 issues are worked on within 1 hour; standard requests within 1 working day. Source: IT Policy FAQ §21 Support.

### Q: What happens to my data when I leave?
Your accounts are disabled on your last day; your manager receives access to business email and files for handover. Personal files must not be stored on company systems; do not copy company data when leaving — this is checked. Source: IT Policy FAQ §22 Exit.

### Q: Can I give a customer access to our systems?
Only to the approved customer-facing portals, with named accounts created by IT on your Reporting Boss's request. Never share your own login. Source: IT Policy FAQ §2 External access.

### Q: What should I do if a customer reports a security incident involving data we shared?
Inform IT Security and Legal immediately with the details; do not discuss liability with the customer. IT Security coordinates the response and any notifications required by contract or law. Source: IT Policy FAQ §7 Customer incidents.

### Q: Is there mandatory security training?
Yes — an annual information-security e-module with an assessment, plus periodic phishing simulations. Completion is tracked by HR and IT. Source: IT Policy FAQ §23 Training.

### Q: What are the rules for video calls with customers?
Use the company's approved meeting platform or the customer's platform; blur or check your background; close confidential documents before sharing your screen; share only the specific window, not the whole desktop. Source: IT Policy FAQ §18 Video calls.

### Q: Who do I contact about the IT and Information Security Policy?
The IT helpdesk for support and access; IT Security for incidents and exceptions. Source: IT Policy FAQ §24 Contacts.
