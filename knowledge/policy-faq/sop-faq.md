# Policy FAQ — Standard Operating Procedures for KAMs

Model SOPs for the FirstGear Compass training programme, consistent with the RFQ to Quotation SOP, the Customer Quality Governance Manual, the Escalation Playbook and the Approval Authority Matrix. Timelines and forms here are illustrative model procedures for training — follow the controlled SOP in the document system if it differs.

Key timelines at a glance: RFQ logged within 1 working day, acknowledged within 2, feasibility go/no-go within 5. Quotation validity 90 days. Complaint acknowledged and containment started within 24 hours; D3 within 24–48 hours; D4 within 7 days; 8D closure within 30 days. Customer visit minutes in the CRM within 2 working days. Payment terms 60 days standard.

### Q: What SOPs does a KAM use most?
RFQ to quotation, quotation approval, sales order and schedule handling, price change and index adjustment, customer complaint and 8D, PPAP and engineering change, sample dispatch, customer claims and credit notes, collections, customer visits and minutes, customer audits, account planning and QBRs, and account handover. Each is summarised in this FAQ. Source: SOP FAQ §1 Overview.

### Q: What is the RFQ intake procedure?
Log the RFQ in the CRM within one working day with an RFQ number, acknowledge receipt to the customer within two working days, confirm the submission deadline, and list missing inputs (drawings, volumes, SOP date, packaging and logistics terms). Do not cost an RFQ until the scope is clear. Source: SOP FAQ §2 RFQ intake.

### Q: How is the RFQ feasibility review run?
The KAM convenes Engineering (technical feasibility, DFM risks), the Plant (capacity, cycle time) and SCM (materials, lead times), with Quality for special characteristics. The review ends in a documented go/no-go decision, normally within five working days. A no-go or conditional feasibility is explained to the customer before any price is discussed. Source: SOP FAQ §3 Feasibility.

### Q: How is a quotation built and approved?
Cost model from documented inputs (Engineering, Plant, SCM, Quality, Finance) → pricing proposal by the KAM → review by the Finance cost controller → approval per the Approval Authority Matrix → submission. The quotation separates piece price, tooling and development cost, states the raw-material index basis and is valid for 90 days. No price is indicated before approval is recorded. Source: SOP FAQ §4 Quotation.

### Q: Who must approve a quotation?
The Head of Key Accounts at or above the 15% margin floor; the CFO below the floor or above ₹50 crore lifetime value; the Managing Director for strategic below-floor quotations. Record the approval in the RFQ file before submission. Source: SOP FAQ §4 Approvals.

### Q: What goes into the RFQ file?
Intake log, feasibility record, cost inputs with source and date, cost model, approval record, submitted quotation and all customer correspondence. Store it in the CRM document folder for the RFQ; files are retained 8 years after the programme ends. Source: SOP FAQ §4 RFQ file.

### Q: What happens after we win (nomination)?
Record the nomination letter or purchase order in the CRM, check it matches the approved quotation (piece price, tooling, volumes, index basis, payment terms), and raise any difference with Finance and Legal before accepting. Then hand over to NPD for the programme kick-off and APQP. Source: SOP FAQ §5 Nomination.

### Q: What if the customer's purchase order differs from our quotation?
Do not accept it as is. List the differences (price, terms, quantities, delivery, warranty, liability) and send them to Finance and Legal; reply to the customer with the corrections within 5 working days. Never let production start on a PO with unapproved terms. Source: SOP FAQ §5 PO review.

### Q: How are customer schedules and call-offs handled?
Customers send monthly forecasts and firm call-offs through their portal or EDI. SCM uploads them to the ERP and confirms capacity. The KAM reviews significant changes (more than ±15% against the forecast) with SCM and the customer, and records agreed changes. Source: SOP FAQ §6 Schedules.

### Q: What is the frozen window for schedules?
Normally the first two weeks of a firm call-off are frozen; changes inside it need SCM's written confirmation before you agree. Changes outside the frozen window follow the normal planning cycle. Customer-specific agreements override this. Source: SOP FAQ §6 Frozen window.

### Q: How do I create a new customer or a new ship-to address?
Raise a customer master request in the ERP workflow with the customer's legal name, GSTIN or tax ID, billing and shipping addresses, contacts and documents (certificate of incorporation, GST certificate). Finance completes the KYC and sets the credit limit before the first sales order. Source: SOP FAQ §7 Customer master.

### Q: How are sales orders processed?
Customer POs or schedule agreements are entered in the ERP by the sales coordinator against the approved price list; the ERP blocks orders without an approved price or with an exceeded credit limit. The KAM resolves blocked orders with Finance. Source: SOP FAQ §8 Sales orders.

### Q: How do I handle a raw-material index price change?
Every quarter Finance calculates the index adjustment (steel, aluminium, copper) using the agreed basis and three-month lag. The KAM sends the calculation to the customer with the supporting index data, agrees the effective date and gets the revised price approved; Finance then updates the price list and issues supplementary invoices or credit notes. Source: SOP FAQ §9 Index adjustment.

### Q: How do I handle the annual price-down negotiation?
Prepare a negotiation brief (objective, walk-away, likely customer position, give-gets, productivity evidence) with Finance, get the approved position before the meeting, never concede beyond it in the room, and record the agreement in writing. Annual price-downs are typically 1–3% against productivity plans. Source: SOP FAQ §10 Price-downs.

### Q: What is the procedure for a customer price challenge?
Listen and capture the ask in writing; do not concede in the meeting; analyse with Finance using cost breakdown and index data; prepare a position with give-gets; obtain approval at the right level; respond by the agreed date. Source: SOP FAQ §10 Price challenges.

### Q: What is the customer complaint procedure?
Acknowledge within 24 hours; start containment within 24 hours (sort stock at the plant, in transit and at the customer); open an 8D with Quality. Share the D3 interim containment within 24–48 hours, root cause (D4) within 7 days, and the full 8D within 30 days unless the customer's requirements are stricter. Register the complaint in the quality system and the CRM. Source: SOP FAQ §11 Complaints.

### Q: Who writes the 8D report?
Quality owns the 8D content with the plant team (D1 team through D8 closure). The KAM makes sure dates are met, reviews the report for clarity before it goes to the customer, and owns all customer communication. Do not speculate on root cause before D4. Source: SOP FAQ §11 8D.

### Q: What if containment cannot start within 24 hours?
Escalate immediately to the Plant Quality Head (Level 1) and, if the customer's line is at risk, to Level 3 (Managing Director and Plant Head). Tell the customer what is being done and when the next update will come. Source: SOP FAQ §11 Escalation.

### Q: How is a customer quality debit or chargeback handled?
Check the debit note against the complaint record, quantities and the agreed sorting rates; get Quality's view on responsibility; agree or dispute it within 15 days. Accepted debits are booked by Finance against the account; disputed ones are tracked in the credit-control list. Source: SOP FAQ §12 Quality debits.

### Q: What is the PPAP submission procedure?
NPD and Quality prepare the PPAP package (default Level 3: full submission with samples) against the customer's requirements; the KAM tracks the submission date, sends it through the customer's portal or SQE and follows up for approval. Parts may not be shipped for production before PPAP approval or a documented customer deviation. Source: SOP FAQ §13 PPAP.

### Q: What if production needs to ship before PPAP approval?
Only with a written customer deviation (temporary approval) specifying quantity, period and conditions, obtained through the customer's SQE. Record it in the quality system; labels must show the deviation number. Source: SOP FAQ §13 Deviations.

### Q: What is the engineering change procedure?
Change request (ECR) → impact assessment (technical, cost, timing) by Engineering, Quality, SCM and Finance → engineering change notice (ECN) → customer approval (usually with PPAP resubmission) → implementation. Customer-initiated changes after design freeze are quoted (price and timing) through the RFQ process before implementation. Source: SOP FAQ §14 Engineering change.

### Q: Can we make a process change without telling the customer?
No. No change to product, process, material, supplier or location may be made without the customer's approval, normally with PPAP resubmission. Unapproved changes are a serious quality-system breach. Source: SOP FAQ §14 Change control.

### Q: How do I send samples to a customer?
Raise a sample request with the part number, quantity, purpose and required date; Quality releases the samples with an inspection report; the plant issues a delivery challan or invoice; logistics packs and ships them. Record the dispatch and tracking number in the CRM. Never hand-carry production samples without a challan. Source: SOP FAQ §15 Samples.

### Q: How are free-of-charge samples approved?
Free samples up to ₹25,000 in value are approved by the Head of Key Accounts; above that, by the CFO. Development samples covered by a tooling or development agreement are invoiced as agreed. Source: SOP FAQ §15 Sample approval.

### Q: How do I handle a customer return of parts?
Get a return material authorisation (RMA) number from Quality before parts come back, agree the reason (quality, excess, wrong part), arrange logistics, and make sure Finance issues the credit note only after the returned quantity is verified at the plant. Source: SOP FAQ §16 Returns.

### Q: How are credit notes and debit notes issued?
The KAM raises a credit-note request in the ERP workflow with the reason and evidence (price correction, quality debit accepted, returned goods); Finance checks and issues it. Credit notes above ₹5 lakh need the Head of Key Accounts' approval, and above ₹25 lakh the CFO's. Source: SOP FAQ §17 Credit notes.

### Q: What is the collections procedure?
Finance publishes the overdue list weekly. The KAM contacts the customer's accounts payable for invoices overdue by more than 15 days, resolves disputes with the owning function, and escalates to the customer's purchasing lead after 30 days overdue. Supplies may be put on credit hold by Finance after 30 days beyond terms, with the Head of Key Accounts informed. Source: SOP FAQ §18 Collections.

### Q: What is a credit hold and who can release it?
A block on new sales orders or dispatches when the customer exceeds its credit limit or has invoices overdue more than 30 days beyond terms. Finance places it; release needs the CFO, normally after a payment or an agreed payment plan. Tell the customer early to avoid a line stop. Source: SOP FAQ §18 Credit hold.

### Q: How do I handle a premium-freight claim?
Establish the cause (our delay or the customer's schedule change) with SCM, get the Head of SCM's approval before premium freight is used, and agree the cost owner with the customer in writing. Claims need documented evidence (schedules, delivery records, invoices). Source: SOP FAQ §19 Premium freight.

### Q: How do I handle a customer warranty claim?
Register it with Quality, request field evidence and parts, agree a joint analysis plan, and do not accept liability until Quality and Legal have reviewed the analysis and the contract's warranty terms. Settlements need CFO approval. Source: SOP FAQ §20 Warranty.

### Q: What is the procedure for a customer visit?
Agree the agenda and attendees in writing, brief the internal team, use approved materials only, record the visit, contacts met and minutes of meeting (MOM) with actions and owners in the CRM within 2 working days, and share the actions internally and with the customer. Source: SOP FAQ §21 Customer visits.

### Q: How do I host a customer audit?
Coordinate with Quality, which leads the audit; confirm the scope, dates and checklist; ensure an NDA covers what will be seen; arrange the plant safety briefing and PPE; attend the opening and closing meetings; track the findings and the corrective-action plan to closure by the due dates. Source: SOP FAQ §22 Customer audits.

### Q: How do I prepare the monthly customer operations review?
Prepare quality (PPM, open complaints and 8D status), delivery (OTIF, risks), commercial (open price changes, claims, overdue invoices) and programme topics with the account team, agree messages internally first, send the agenda 2 days ahead, and publish minutes within 2 working days. Source: SOP FAQ §23 Monthly review.

### Q: How do I prepare a quarterly business review (QBR)?
With the Reporting Boss: account performance versus plan, quality and delivery trends, programme status, the RFQ pipeline, strategic topics (EV programmes, capacity, localisation) and asks. Use the approved corporate presentation for company information. Rehearse internally one week ahead. Source: SOP FAQ §24 QBR.

### Q: What is the annual account plan?
A document submitted in April covering the customer's strategy, our share of wallet, AOP revenue and margin, the RFQ pipeline, programme timelines, stakeholder map, risks and mitigations, the governance calendar and KPI targets. Update it quarterly. Source: SOP FAQ §25 Account plan.

### Q: How do I keep the CRM up to date?
Record every customer contact, visit and MOM within 2 working days; keep RFQs and opportunities with the right stage, value and dates; maintain the stakeholder map; attach key documents. CRM data drives the RFQ hit-rate KPI and your manager's reviews. Source: SOP FAQ §26 CRM.

### Q: How is an account handed over between KAMs?
A written handover note (account plan, open RFQs and quotations, open commitments and 8Ds, pending claims, overdue receivables, stakeholder map, risks), a joint internal review with the account team, and a joint introduction meeting with key customer contacts. Both KAMs and the Reporting Boss sign the note. Source: SOP FAQ §27 Handover.

### Q: How do I request extra capacity for a customer?
Raise a capacity request with the volumes, timing and part numbers; SCM and the Plant assess it and propose options (overtime, alternate line, investment). Investment proposals go through capital approval; commit to the customer only after the plan is approved. Source: SOP FAQ §28 Capacity.

### Q: How is customer tooling handled?
Tooling is quoted separately, purchased against the customer's tooling PO, and invoiced at PPAP approval or as agreed. Customer-owned tools are tagged and recorded in the asset register; their maintenance and replacement terms follow the contract. Source: SOP FAQ §29 Tooling.

### Q: How is a packaging change handled?
Packaging is part of the PPAP. Changes requested by the customer or proposed by us go through the engineering change procedure, with SCM costing and customer approval before implementation. Source: SOP FAQ §30 Packaging.

### Q: How do I handle a delivery risk?
Confirm the facts with SCM and the Plant (quantity at risk, dates, cause), agree a recovery plan (overtime, alternate line, partial shipments, premium freight), and inform the customer early with the plan and the time of the next update. Never promise a date that SCM and the Plant have not confirmed in writing. Source: SOP FAQ §31 Delivery risk.

### Q: How do I respond to a customer's supplier self-assessment or questionnaire?
Log it in the CRM, route sections to the owning functions (Quality, EHS, Finance, IT, HR, Legal), consolidate the answers, get them reviewed by the Head of Key Accounts and submit before the deadline. Use only approved information. Source: SOP FAQ §32 Questionnaires.

### Q: How do I handle a customer's request for our cost breakdown?
Cost breakdowns are shared only as agreed in the contract or quotation format, after Finance prepares and the Head of Key Accounts approves the version. Never share internal cost models, overhead rates or margin. Source: SOP FAQ §33 Cost breakdowns.

### Q: What is the procedure for a letter of intent (LOI)?
Send the LOI to Legal and Finance for review; check it matches the approved quotation; get it signed only by an authorised signatory. Do not start tooling or development spend until the LOI or PO is accepted. Source: SOP FAQ §34 LOI.

### Q: How do I handle an export order?
Confirm Incoterms, currency, payment terms and documentation with Finance and SCM logistics; check export-control and sanctions screening with Legal for new destinations; logistics prepares the commercial invoice, packing list and shipping documents. Source: SOP FAQ §35 Exports.

### Q: How are customer portals managed?
Each customer portal (schedules, quality, invoicing) has named users approved by the KAM; credentials are personal and never shared. Remove users who leave or change roles within 2 working days. Source: SOP FAQ §36 Customer portals.

### Q: What is the procedure when a customer contact changes?
Update the stakeholder map and CRM contacts, inform the account team, arrange an introduction meeting, and check portal and distribution-list access. Source: SOP FAQ §26 CRM.

### Q: How do I raise an internal escalation when a function is late?
Raise it first with the function lead, then at the weekly internal account review, then with the Head of Key Accounts. Record the escalation and the agreed action. Source: SOP FAQ §37 Internal escalation.

### Q: Where are the controlled SOPs kept?
In the document management system and, for the key ones, in the Compass knowledge base with version and effective date. Use only the current version; printed copies are uncontrolled. Source: SOP FAQ §38 Document control.

### Q: Who do I contact about the KAM SOPs?
Your Mentor during onboarding, the Head of Key Accounts for commercial SOPs, and the Head of Quality for quality SOPs. Source: SOP FAQ §39 Contacts.
