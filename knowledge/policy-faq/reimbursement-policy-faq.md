# Policy FAQ — Reimbursement and Expense Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Limits and timelines here are illustrative model policies for training — confirm current values with Finance before incurring an expense.

Key rules at a glance: claim through the expense module in the employee portal within 30 days of the expense (15 days for travel). Claims older than 60 days need CFO approval. Bills must be in the company name with the company GSTIN where GST applies. Approval by your Reporting Boss, then Finance; payment within 10 working days of Finance approval. Mobile and internet: G2–G3 up to ₹1,500 a month, G4 and above ₹2,000. Customer entertainment needs prior approval, up to ₹2,500 per head (higher with Head of Key Accounts approval), never for public officials, no alcohol reimbursed.

### Q: What expenses can I claim?
Business expenses incurred for the company that are not paid directly by the company: travel and local conveyance, approved customer entertainment, mobile and internet within limits, approved training and membership fees, small purchases approved by your Reporting Boss, and courier or document charges in emergencies. Personal expenses are never reimbursed. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: How do I submit a claim?
In the expense module of the employee portal: choose the expense type, date and amount, attach the bill (photo or PDF), add the business purpose and customer or CRM reference, and submit. One claim can hold many lines; travel claims must be linked to the approved travel request. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §2 Submitting.

### Q: What is the deadline for claims?
Within 30 days of the expense, or within 15 days of returning from a trip. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the expense are not paid unless the CFO approves the reason. Year-end (March) expenses must be claimed by 10 April for the accounts close. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §3 Deadlines.

### Q: Who approves my claims?
Your Reporting Boss approves the business need; Finance (Accounts Payable) checks bills, limits and tax compliance. Claims above ₹50,000 in one submission also need the function head. You cannot approve your own claim. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §4 Approvals.

### Q: When will I be paid?
Within 10 working days after Finance approves, credited to your salary account. The status is visible in the expense module at every step. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §5 Payment.

### Q: What makes a bill valid?
A legible original or digital invoice showing the supplier's name, date, description, amount and, where GST applies, the supplier's GSTIN and invoice number. For hotels, airlines and services above ₹5,000, the invoice must be in the company name with the company GSTIN so the company can claim input tax credit. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §6 Bills.

### Q: Why does the company GSTIN matter on hotel bills?
GST invoices in the company name with its GSTIN let the company recover the GST as input tax credit. Give the GSTIN of the state where you stay (the list is in the expense module) at hotel check-in. Bills without it are still reimbursed but the lost credit is reported to your Reporting Boss. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §6 GST.

### Q: What if I lose a bill?
Submit a self-declaration for up to ₹500 per claim, at most twice a quarter. For larger amounts, get a duplicate invoice from the supplier; hotels and airlines can always re-issue invoices. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §6 Lost bills.

### Q: How much mobile and internet expense can I claim?
KAMs and other field roles in G2–G3 can claim up to ₹1,500 a month and G4 and above up to ₹2,000 a month against the postpaid bill or recharge receipt, if a company SIM is not provided. If you use a company SIM, data and calls are paid by the company directly and nothing is claimed. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §7 Mobile.

### Q: Is there a handset allowance?
G2 KAMs who use their own handset with the company SIM may claim a handset allowance of up to ₹20,000 once every 3 years against the purchase invoice. G3 and above are issued a company handset. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §7 Handset.

### Q: Can I claim home internet?
Yes, up to ₹1,000 a month for employees approved to work from home regularly, within the overall mobile and internet limit. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §7 Internet.

### Q: What is customer entertainment and how is it approved?
Meals or hospitality for customer employees with a clear business purpose (for example a dinner after a review). Get prior approval from your Reporting Boss by email or in the portal, keep it modest, and record the names, companies and purpose in the claim. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §8 Customer entertainment.

### Q: What is the limit for customer entertainment?
Up to ₹2,500 per head (including company participants) per occasion with Reporting Boss approval; above that, the Head of Key Accounts must approve in advance. Entertainment for the same customer contact more than once a quarter needs Head of Key Accounts approval. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §8 Limits.

### Q: Can I entertain a government official or a public-sector customer?
Not without written approval from Legal and Compliance in advance. Gifts, meals or hospitality to public officials are high-risk under anti-bribery law and the Code of Conduct. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §8 Public officials.

### Q: Is alcohol reimbursed?
No. Alcohol is not reimbursed in any claim, including customer entertainment; it must be paid personally if ordered. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §8 Alcohol.

### Q: Can I give a gift to a customer and claim it?
Only company-branded items from Admin's approved stock, or other items up to ₹2,000 per recipient approved by your Reporting Boss, for a recognised occasion such as a festival. Cash, vouchers or anything that could influence a decision are never allowed. See the Compliance and Ethics FAQ. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §9 Gifts.

### Q: Can I claim team lunches or celebrations?
Team events are funded from the department budget with the function head's approval and are claimed by the organiser with the attendee list, up to ₹1,000 per head. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §10 Team events.

### Q: Are training courses or certifications reimbursed?
Yes, if approved by your Reporting Boss and HR before you enrol, and relevant to your role. Courses over ₹50,000 need a service commitment as in the HR Policy. Reimbursement is on the invoice and proof of completion. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §11 Learning.

### Q: Are professional memberships reimbursed?
Memberships of professional bodies relevant to your role (for example an industry association or a professional institute) are reimbursed once a year with Reporting Boss and HR approval. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §11 Memberships.

### Q: Can I buy small items for work and claim them?
Small items up to ₹2,000 (for example adapters or presentation materials for a customer visit) with your Reporting Boss's approval. Anything else goes through Procurement or the Admin and IT helpdesks — do not buy IT equipment or software yourself. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §12 Purchases.

### Q: What expenses are never reimbursed?
Personal meals at base, alcohol, fines and traffic challans, personal travel, family expenses, gym or spa, minibar, personal phone accessories, upgrades without approval, donations, gifts to colleagues, and anything without a business purpose. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §13 Non-reimbursable items.

### Q: Can I claim expenses incurred by a colleague?
No. Each person claims their own expenses. For a shared bill (for example a customer dinner), the senior-most company person present pays and claims, listing all attendees. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §14 Shared expenses.

### Q: What if my claim is partly rejected?
Finance records the reason for each rejected line. You can correct and resubmit within 15 days, or discuss it with your Reporting Boss. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §15 Rejections.

### Q: How do advances work with reimbursements?
Travel advances (see the Travel Policy FAQ) are adjusted against your claim. If expenses are less than the advance, the balance is recovered from your next salary unless you refund it within 7 days. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §16 Advances.

### Q: Are reimbursements taxable?
Business reimbursements supported by bills are not taxable. Fixed allowances paid without bills, and some items such as mobile handsets above certain limits, may be treated as taxable salary under income-tax rules; payroll will tell you if this applies. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §17 Tax.

### Q: Can I pay a supplier personally and claim the amount?
No, except small emergency purchases under ₹2,000. Supplier payments must go through a purchase order and Accounts Payable so GST, TDS and approvals are handled correctly. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §12 Purchases.

### Q: What happens if I submit a false or inflated claim?
It is treated as fraud under the Code of Conduct and leads to disciplinary action, which can include termination and recovery. Finance audits claims regularly, including random samples and data analytics on mileage and entertainment. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §18 Audit and misuse.

### Q: Does Finance audit expense claims?
Yes. Every claim is checked against policy, and a random sample is audited each month with original bills. Repeated policy breaches are reported to the function head. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §18 Audit and misuse.

### Q: Can a KAM in onboarding claim customer entertainment?
No. Customer entertainment needs customer-facing authority, which starts after the Day-30 sign-off; during Phase 2, customer meals are hosted and claimed by the Mentor or Reporting Boss accompanying you. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §8 Onboarding.

### Q: Are relocation expenses reimbursed?
For company-initiated transfers, yes — see the HR Policy FAQ on transfers: travel for the family, shifting of household goods by an approved vendor, and up to 15 days of temporary accommodation, claimed with bills within 60 days of moving. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §19 Relocation.

### Q: Can I claim medical expenses?
Medical costs are covered by the group mediclaim, not by reimbursement claims. Claim hospitalisation through the insurer's cashless network or the insurer's reimbursement process. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §20 Medical.

### Q: Are visa, passport or vaccination fees reimbursed?
Visa fees and mandatory vaccinations for business trips are reimbursed. Passport fees are not reimbursed, except an emergency passport renewal requested for business travel. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §21 Travel documents.

### Q: How should expenses be coded?
The expense tool assigns your cost centre automatically. For customer-related expenses, select the customer code so the cost is visible in account profitability. Wrong customer codes distort the KPI reports, so check before submitting. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §22 Coding.

### Q: Can I claim expenses in foreign currency?
Yes. Enter the foreign amount and currency; the tool converts it at the forex card rate or, for card payments, the bank statement rate. Attach the card statement for non-forex-card payments. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §23 Foreign currency.

### Q: Is the company credit card available?
Corporate cards are issued to G4 and above who travel frequently. Card spends are reconciled in the expense module within 15 days of the statement; personal spends must be repaid immediately. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §24 Corporate cards.

### Q: Who checks that limits are applied correctly?
The expense module applies grade limits automatically and flags exceptions; your Reporting Boss sees the flags when approving; Finance enforces policy before payment. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §4 Approvals.

### Q: Can my Reporting Boss approve an exception to the limits?
Only within the exception limits in this policy (for example 25% above a hotel cap with a reason). Anything beyond needs the function head and, for amounts above ₹1 lakh, the CFO. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §25 Exceptions.

### Q: How long should I keep original bills?
Upload digital copies in the expense module; keep originals for 12 months or until Finance confirms the audit sample, whichever is later. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §26 Records.

### Q: Who do I contact about the Reimbursement Policy?
Finance (Accounts Payable) through the expense module helpdesk; your Reporting Boss for approvals. Source: Reimbursement Policy FAQ §27 Contacts.
