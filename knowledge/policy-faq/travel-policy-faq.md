# Policy FAQ — Travel and Conveyance Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Entitlements, rates and limits here are illustrative model policies for training — confirm current values with the Admin travel desk or Finance before booking.

Key rules at a glance (domestic, per night including taxes; daily allowance per day):
- Mode: air economy for G2 and above when the one-way journey is more than 400 km or more than 6 hours by road or rail; G1 travels by AC 3-tier train or AC bus, and by air only with approval for journeys over 800 km. AC 2-tier train for G2 and above.
- Hotel caps, Class A cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad): G1 ₹4,000 · G2 ₹6,000 · G3 ₹7,500 · G4 ₹9,000 · G5 actuals. Class B cities (other state capitals and cities above 10 lakh population): G1 ₹3,000 · G2 ₹4,500 · G3 ₹5,500 · G4 ₹7,000. Class C (all other places): G1 ₹2,000 · G2 ₹3,000 · G3 ₹4,000 · G4 ₹5,000.
- Daily allowance (food and incidentals, no bills): G1 ₹700 · G2 ₹1,000 · G3 ₹1,200 · G4 ₹1,500 · G5 ₹2,000.
- Book through the Admin travel desk at least 7 days ahead for air; travel requests are approved by the Reporting Boss.

### Q: What counts as official travel?
Travel away from your base location for business: customer visits, plant visits, supplier visits, audits, trade fairs, training and internal reviews. Travel within your base city (or within 100 km of it) is local conveyance — see the Local Conveyance Policy FAQ. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: How do I request official travel?
Raise a travel request in the employee portal with the purpose, customer or plant, dates, mode and estimated cost, and link the CRM visit or opportunity where relevant. Your Reporting Boss approves it; the request then goes to the Admin travel desk for booking. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §2 Travel request.

### Q: How far in advance should I book?
At least 7 days ahead for domestic air, 3 days for rail and 21 days for international travel. Late bookings need a reason (for example a customer escalation) and cost more, so plan visits in your account plan. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §2 Booking.

### Q: Can I book my own tickets?
Book through the Admin travel desk so the company's negotiated fares and GST invoices are used. Self-booking is allowed only in an emergency or outside office hours; then book the lowest logical fare, use the company GSTIN for the invoice and inform the travel desk the next working day. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §2 Booking.

### Q: Which class can I fly in?
Economy class on domestic flights for all grades. Choose the lowest logical fare — the cheapest fare within two hours of your preferred time on a reasonable routing. Premium seats, extra baggage and upgrades are at your own cost unless approved for a business reason (for example carrying samples). Source: Travel Policy FAQ §3 Air travel.

### Q: When is a KAM allowed to fly instead of taking the train?
Grade G2 and above may fly economy when the one-way journey is more than 400 km or more than 6 hours by road or rail. For shorter trips, use AC 2-tier rail, AC chair car or a taxi as per the Local Conveyance Policy for intercity distances under 250 km. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §3 Mode entitlement.

### Q: What rail class am I entitled to?
AC 2-tier for G2 and above; AC 3-tier for G1. AC chair car or Vande Bharat chair car for daytime journeys up to 8 hours. Executive chair car is allowed for G4 and above. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §3 Rail travel.

### Q: Can I travel by my own car to another city?
Yes, for intercity trips up to 250 km one way, at the own-vehicle rate in the Local Conveyance Policy (₹12 per km for a car), plus tolls and parking with receipts, provided it costs no more than the entitled mode. Self-driving is limited to 300 km per day for safety; you must not drive after a night flight or for more than 4 hours without a break. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §4 Own vehicle.

### Q: Can I hire a taxi for an intercity trip?
Yes, for trips up to 250 km one way where rail or bus is impractical (for example to a customer plant without a station), using a sedan from the company's approved car-rental vendors booked through the travel desk. Sharing with colleagues on the same trip is expected. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §4 Hired taxi.

### Q: What are the hotel limits?
Per night including taxes: Class A cities — G1 ₹4,000, G2 ₹6,000, G3 ₹7,500, G4 ₹9,000, G5 actuals; Class B — G1 ₹3,000, G2 ₹4,500, G3 ₹5,500, G4 ₹7,000; Class C — G1 ₹2,000, G2 ₹3,000, G3 ₹4,000, G4 ₹5,000. Use company-empanelled hotels where available; they often include breakfast and invoice the company directly. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §5 Hotels.

### Q: Which cities are Class A?
Delhi NCR (including Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad), Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata and Ahmedabad. Class B covers other state capitals and cities with more than 10 lakh population; everything else is Class C. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §5 City classes.

### Q: What if no hotel is available within my limit?
Ask the travel desk to find options; if none are available within the cap (for example during a trade fair), your Reporting Boss can approve up to 25% above the cap with a reason in the travel request. Anything higher needs the function head. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §5 Exceptions.

### Q: Can I stay at a company guest house?
Yes, and it is preferred where available (for example near the Pune and Chennai plants). Book through the travel desk. When you stay at a guest house where meals are provided, half the daily allowance applies. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §5 Guest houses.

### Q: Can I stay with friends or family instead of a hotel?
Yes. You may claim a lodging allowance of ₹1,000 per night (G1–G4) without bills instead of hotel costs, plus the normal daily allowance. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §5 Own arrangements.

### Q: What is the daily allowance?
A fixed amount per day for food, local tips and incidentals, with no bills needed: G1 ₹700, G2 ₹1,000, G3 ₹1,200, G4 ₹1,500, G5 ₹2,000. It is paid for each day away from base on business. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §6 Daily allowance.

### Q: How is the daily allowance calculated for short trips?
Full allowance for each 24-hour period away from base and for any part-day of more than 12 hours; half allowance for absences of 6–12 hours; nothing for less than 6 hours (local conveyance applies). Days are counted from departure to return. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §6 Daily allowance.

### Q: Is the daily allowance reduced if meals are provided?
Yes. If the customer, the hotel package or the company provides lunch and dinner, half the daily allowance applies for that day. Breakfast included in the hotel tariff does not reduce it. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §6 Daily allowance.

### Q: Can I claim actual meal bills instead of the daily allowance?
No, the daily allowance replaces meal claims for yourself. Meals with customers are claimed separately as customer entertainment, with prior approval and the attendee list, under the Reimbursement Policy. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §6 Daily allowance.

### Q: Can I get a travel advance?
Yes, up to 75% of the estimated cost (hotel, daily allowance and local travel) for trips of 3 days or more, requested in the travel request. Tickets booked by the travel desk are not included. Settle the advance in your expense claim within 7 days of returning; you cannot take a new advance while one is outstanding for more than 15 days. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §7 Advances.

### Q: How do I claim travel expenses?
Submit the expense claim in the employee portal within 15 days of returning, linked to the approved travel request, with hotel invoices (GST invoice in the company name), taxi and toll receipts and the boarding pass for self-booked flights. Your Reporting Boss approves, then Finance pays to your salary account within 10 working days. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §8 Claims.

### Q: Are airport transfers reimbursed?
Yes, taxi or app cab fares to and from the airport or station at actuals with receipts. If you drive your own car to the airport, parking is reimbursed for up to 3 days; for longer trips use a taxi. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §9 Transfers.

### Q: What local travel can I claim in the city I visit?
App cabs or taxis at actuals with e-receipts (economy category), auto-rickshaws or metro up to ₹300 a day without receipts on a self-declaration, and a hired car through the travel desk for full-day customer or plant visits. See the Local Conveyance Policy FAQ. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §9 Local travel on tour.

### Q: What expenses are not reimbursed on travel?
Minibar, alcohol, spa, laundry for trips under 3 days, personal calls, in-room movies, fines and traffic challans, excess baggage for personal items, upgrades, travel insurance you buy yourself (the company covers you), and costs for family members. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §10 Non-reimbursable items.

### Q: Is laundry reimbursed?
Yes, for trips longer than 3 nights, up to ₹500 per day of stay beyond the third night, with the hotel bill. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §10 Laundry.

### Q: What happens if a trip is cancelled or changed?
Cancel or change the booking through the travel desk as soon as you know. Cancellation charges are borne by the company when the change is for a business reason recorded in the travel request; charges caused by personal reasons are recovered from you. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §11 Changes.

### Q: Can I extend a business trip for personal days?
Yes, with your Reporting Boss's approval, if the return fare does not increase (or you pay the difference). Hotel and daily allowance stop on the last business day; apply for leave for the personal days. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §12 Combined travel.

### Q: Can my family travel with me on a business trip?
Yes, at your own cost. The company pays only your entitled travel and the single-occupancy hotel rate. Family members may not join customer meetings or plant visits. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §12 Family.

### Q: Does a new KAM travel during onboarding?
In Phase 1 (Days 1–15) travel is limited to plant walks and internal sessions arranged with your Mentor. From Phase 2 you may accompany your Mentor or Reporting Boss on customer visits as an observer; independent customer travel starts after the relevant gates and Reporting Boss approvals. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §13 Onboarding.

### Q: How do I plan a customer visit trip?
Combine visits by region, confirm meeting times in writing with the customer, book through the travel desk at least 7 days ahead, carry approved materials only, record the visit and minutes of meeting (MOM) in the CRM within 2 working days, and share action points with the internal team. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §14 Customer visits.

### Q: Who approves international travel?
Your Reporting Boss and the function head, then the CFO for G1–G3; for G4 and above, the function head and the CFO. Submit the request at least 21 days before travel with the business case and estimated cost. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 International travel.

### Q: Which class can I fly internationally?
Economy for all grades on flights up to 6 hours; premium economy for G4 on flights over 6 hours; business class for G5 on flights over 6 hours. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 International air.

### Q: What are the international hotel and per diem limits?
Hotels per night: Europe, North America and Japan — G1–G3 USD 150, G4 USD 180, G5 actuals; rest of Asia, Middle East, Mexico and others — G1–G3 USD 120, G4 USD 150. Per diem for meals and incidentals: Europe and North America G1–G3 USD 75, G4–G5 USD 100; elsewhere G1–G3 USD 60, G4–G5 USD 80. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 International limits.

### Q: How do I get foreign exchange for an international trip?
Request a prepaid forex card through the Admin travel desk with the approved travel request at least 5 working days ahead; it is loaded with the per diem and an estimate of hotel and local travel. Settle the card balance within 7 days of returning; unused forex is returned. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 Forex.

### Q: Am I insured when travelling abroad?
Yes. The company's overseas travel insurance covers medical emergencies, evacuation and baggage loss for business trips. Admin issues the certificate with your tickets; carry it with you. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 Insurance.

### Q: What should I do about visas?
Apply through the Admin travel desk at least 30 days before travel; it provides the company letter, customer invitation support and appointment help. Visa fees are reimbursed. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 Visas.

### Q: Do the export-control and confidentiality rules apply abroad?
Yes. Carry only approved presentations; do not carry customer drawings unless needed and encrypted; never connect to unknown Wi-Fi without VPN; and check with Legal before discussing technology with entities in sanctioned countries. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §15 Compliance abroad.

### Q: Do I earn frequent-flyer miles?
You may keep personal loyalty points, but they must never influence the choice of airline or hotel — the lowest logical fare and empanelled hotels apply. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §16 Loyalty programmes.

### Q: What if I have an emergency while travelling?
Call the 24×7 travel-desk helpline (on the back of your ID card) and your Reporting Boss. For medical emergencies abroad, call the insurer's assistance number on the certificate first. Changes needed for safety are always approved. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §17 Emergencies.

### Q: Are travel days counted as working days?
Yes, travel on working days for business is working time. Travel on a weekly off alone does not earn a compensatory off; see the Leave Policy FAQ. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §18 Travel time.

### Q: What if I have to travel at night or very early?
Avoid driving yourself at night. If a flight lands after 22:00 or departs before 6:00, an app cab to and from home is reimbursed even if you would normally drive, and a hotel near the airport may be approved for very early departures. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §18 Late or early travel.

### Q: Can I claim if I lost a receipt?
Submit a self-declaration for up to ₹500 per claim, at most twice a quarter. Hotel and air invoices can always be re-issued, so ask the hotel or the travel desk for a duplicate rather than declaring. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §8 Lost receipts.

### Q: What happens if I claim something not allowed?
Finance rejects the line or recovers it. Deliberately false claims are a Code of Conduct breach and lead to disciplinary action. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §19 Compliance.

### Q: Who do I contact about the Travel Policy?
The Admin travel desk for bookings; Finance (Accounts Payable) for claims and advances; your Reporting Boss for approvals. Source: Travel Policy FAQ §20 Contacts.
