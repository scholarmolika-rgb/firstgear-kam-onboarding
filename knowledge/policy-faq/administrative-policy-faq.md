# Policy FAQ — Administrative Policy

Model policy for the FirstGear Compass training programme. Administrative rules, amounts and timelines here are illustrative model policies for training — confirm with the Admin helpdesk before acting.

Key rules at a glance: requests go through the Admin helpdesk in the employee portal. ID cards are worn at all times; a lost card is reported within 24 hours. KAMs are issued a laptop and a company mobile connection on Day 1 against an asset acknowledgement. Customer visitors are pre-registered at least 24 hours ahead. Samples and documents leave only through the mailroom or logistics with the proper paperwork.

### Q: What does the Administration department handle?
Facilities and office space, ID and access cards, company assets (other than IT configuration), travel desk bookings, hotel tie-ups, visiting cards, stationery, courier and mailroom, meeting rooms, visitor management, canteen, transport, pool vehicles, housekeeping and security coordination. IT handles devices' software, email and system access. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §1 Scope.

### Q: How do I raise an Admin request?
Use the Admin helpdesk in the employee portal: pick the category (ID card, visiting cards, stationery, courier, travel desk, meeting room, pool vehicle, facilities) and submit. Routine requests are closed within 2 working days; urgent travel or visitor requests can be escalated to the Admin desk by phone. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §2 Requests.

### Q: When do I get my ID card?
On your first day, after HR completes joining formalities. Until then you receive a temporary card valid for 7 days. The ID card also works as your access card at headquarters. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §3 ID cards.

### Q: Do I have to wear my ID card?
Yes, visibly, at all times on company premises, including plants and warehouses. Security may refuse entry without it. Do not lend your card to anyone. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §3 ID cards.

### Q: What if I lose my ID card?
Report it to Security and the Admin helpdesk within 24 hours so the card is deactivated. A replacement is issued within 2 working days; a ₹500 replacement charge applies for a second loss within a year. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §3 ID cards.

### Q: Which assets will a KAM be issued?
A laptop with standard software, a company mobile connection (CUG SIM) with a data plan, a laptop bag and, on request, a portable data device for travel. You sign an asset acknowledgement in the portal. A company phone handset is issued to G3 and above; G2 KAMs may use their own handset with the company SIM and claim the handset allowance in the Reimbursement Policy. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §4 Assets.

### Q: What are my responsibilities for company assets?
Keep them secure, use them for company work, do not install unapproved software, never leave a laptop unattended in a car or public place, and report loss, theft or damage immediately. Loss caused by negligence may be recovered as per the written-down value. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §4 Assets.

### Q: What do I do if I lose my laptop or it is stolen?
Report it to IT Security within 2 hours (so it can be remotely locked and wiped) and to Admin; file a police complaint (FIR or online e-report) within 24 hours and upload a copy. A replacement is issued after the report. Customer data on the device is a security incident — see the IT and Information Security FAQ. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §4 Loss of assets.

### Q: How do I order visiting cards?
Request them in the Admin helpdesk; the design, title and contact details follow the approved company format, and your designation is taken from HR records. 100 cards are printed per order and delivered within 5 working days. New KAMs receive cards after the Day-30 sign-off, when they begin customer-facing work; until then, introduce yourself with the Mentor. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §5 Visiting cards.

### Q: How do I get stationery?
Request it through the Admin helpdesk or collect it from the floor stationery cabinet; bulk or special items need your Reporting Boss's approval. Personal use of company stationery or printing is not allowed. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §6 Stationery.

### Q: How do I book a meeting room?
Book it through the calendar system, selecting the room as a resource. Customer meetings get priority for the customer meeting rooms on the ground floor. Release the booking if your meeting is cancelled; rooms not used within 15 minutes are released automatically. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §7 Meeting rooms.

### Q: How do I invite a customer to headquarters?
Pre-register the visitor in the visitor-management system at least 24 hours ahead (name, company, purpose, host, time), book a customer meeting room and request refreshments through the Admin helpdesk. Visitors receive a QR pass, sign the visitor register and must be escorted by the host at all times. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §8 Visitors.

### Q: Can a customer visit our plant?
Yes, with the Plant Head's approval. Register the visit at least 48 hours ahead, have a non-disclosure agreement in place where drawings or processes will be seen, arrange the plant safety briefing and PPE, and remember that photography is not allowed without written permission. The Quality team usually co-hosts customer audits. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §8 Plant visits.

### Q: How do I send documents or samples to a customer?
Use the mailroom for documents and the logistics desk for samples. Samples need a quality release (inspection report), a delivery challan or invoice from the plant, and correct packaging and labelling. Never carry production samples in personal baggage without a challan. Export samples need customs documents arranged by SCM. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §9 Courier and samples.

### Q: What courier service do we use?
Admin has contracts with approved courier and express providers; book through the mailroom so the shipment is tracked and charged to your cost centre. Personal courier bookings are not reimbursed except in an emergency approved by your Reporting Boss. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §9 Courier and samples.

### Q: Is there a pool car for plant visits?
Yes. Book a pool car through the Admin helpdesk at least 24 hours ahead for plant or customer visits within the region. Pool cars come with a driver; share the car where colleagues travel the same route. For longer distances, see the Travel Policy FAQ. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §10 Pool vehicles.

### Q: Is there company transport to the plants?
Company buses run between the city and the Gurugram and Manesar plants on fixed routes at shift timings; Pune and Chennai plants have their own routes. Office staff visiting a plant may use the bus with their ID card, subject to seat availability. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §10 Transport.

### Q: Is parking available at headquarters?
Yes, for employees with a registered vehicle sticker, on a first-come basis; two-wheeler and four-wheeler areas are separate. Visitor parking is reserved for pre-registered customers. Register your vehicle through the Admin helpdesk. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §11 Parking.

### Q: Is there a canteen?
Yes. The headquarters and plant canteens serve subsidised breakfast and lunch; meal charges are deducted from salary based on card usage. Customer lunches can be ordered through the Admin helpdesk and charged to your cost centre. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §12 Canteen.

### Q: What is the clean-desk policy?
Lock away customer documents, drawings and price information when you leave your desk, lock your screen (Windows key + L) whenever you step away, and clear the desk at the end of the day. Printouts with confidential information must be collected immediately and shredded when no longer needed. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §13 Clean desk.

### Q: How do I dispose of confidential documents?
Use the locked shred bins on each floor. Never put drawings, quotations or customer data into general waste. Large volumes can be collected by Admin for certified shredding. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §13 Disposal.

### Q: How long must I keep customer and commercial records?
Contracts, quotations, approval records and RFQ files are kept for 8 years after the programme ends; 8D reports and PPAP records for the life of the part plus one year or as the customer requires; general correspondence for 3 years. Keep records in the approved systems (CRM, document management), not on personal drives. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §14 Records retention.

### Q: Can I use the company address for personal deliveries?
No. Personal parcels at the office are not accepted by the mailroom except for occasional items held at reception at your own risk. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §15 Personal use.

### Q: What should I do in a fire or emergency?
Follow the evacuation signs to the assembly point, do not use lifts, follow the floor marshals and wait for the head count. Fire drills are held twice a year. Report any hazard to the Admin or EHS team. Emergency numbers are on the back of your ID card. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §16 Emergencies.

### Q: Who handles office maintenance problems?
Raise a facilities ticket in the Admin helpdesk for air-conditioning, lighting, furniture, washrooms or cleaning. Urgent issues (leaks, electrical faults) can be reported to the facility desk by phone. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §17 Facilities.

### Q: Can I get a desk phone or a headset?
Hot-desking areas have shared phones; dedicated desk phones are issued to G4 and above. Headsets for online meetings are issued on request through the IT helpdesk. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §4 Assets.

### Q: Is there a lost-and-found?
Yes, at the reception or security desk. Items are kept for 30 days and then disposed of or donated. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §18 Lost and found.

### Q: How do I get a guest-house or company accommodation?
Company guest houses near the Pune and Chennai plants can be booked through the Admin travel desk for official visits, subject to availability; they are preferred over hotels when available. See the Travel Policy FAQ for entitlements. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §19 Guest houses.

### Q: Who arranges corporate gifts for customers?
Admin keeps a stock of approved company-branded items (diaries, calendars, small branded items) for festivals and customer visits. Request them through the Admin helpdesk with the customer name and occasion. Only approved branded items within the Code of Conduct limits may be given — see the Compliance and Ethics FAQ. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §20 Corporate gifts.

### Q: How do I arrange a dinner or event for a customer?
Get prior approval from your Reporting Boss for customer entertainment (see the Reimbursement Policy FAQ for limits), then ask the Admin helpdesk to book a company-empanelled venue so the invoice comes to the company. Record attendees and business purpose. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §20 Customer events.

### Q: Can I use the company letterhead?
Only for official communication within your role. Letters that create a commitment (prices, delivery dates, warranties, letters of comfort) must be approved as per the Approval Authority Matrix and signed by an authorised signatory — a KAM does not sign such letters. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §21 Letterhead.

### Q: Who can sign contracts or purchase orders on behalf of the company?
Only authorised signatories under the board-approved delegation of authority. KAMs do not sign customer contracts, agreements or acceptance of customer purchase orders with special terms; they prepare them for review by Legal and Finance and for signature by the authorised person. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §21 Signing authority.

### Q: What is the policy on photographs and videos in the office?
Photography inside plants, labs and production areas is not allowed without written permission from the Plant Head. Office photos for internal communication are fine, but never photograph screens, drawings or whiteboards with confidential information. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §22 Photography.

### Q: Can I bring my own furniture or equipment?
No personal electrical equipment (heaters, kettles, chargers other than standard phone chargers) may be used on the premises for safety reasons. Ergonomic chairs or monitor stands can be requested through Admin with a medical note. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §17 Facilities.

### Q: Is smoking allowed?
Only in designated smoking zones outside the buildings. Smoking, vaping and tobacco are prohibited inside offices, plants, vehicles and near any storage area. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §23 Smoking.

### Q: What is the procedure when I leave the company?
Return the laptop, mobile SIM, ID card, access card, pool-car fuel card if any, and any documents to Admin and IT, and get the clearance signed in the portal before your last day. Unreturned assets are recovered from the final settlement. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §24 Exit clearance.

### Q: Who handles hotel and travel bookings?
The Admin travel desk books air, rail and hotels for official travel through the travel request in the portal. See the Travel and Conveyance Policy FAQ for entitlements and approvals. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §25 Travel desk.

### Q: How do I get a visa invitation or support letter?
Request it through the Admin travel desk at least 30 days before travel with your travel approval, passport copy and the customer invitation if any. Admin arranges the company letter and visa appointment support. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §25 Visa support.

### Q: Who do I contact about the Administrative Policy?
The Admin helpdesk in the employee portal, or the Head of Administration for unresolved issues. Source: Administrative Policy FAQ §26 Contacts.
