# Manufacturing Process Overview

FirstGear is the training alias of an NSE/BSE-listed mobility-technology company. Sections 1 and 7 use the company's publicly reported footprint. Sections 2–6 describe the standard practice this programme teaches for these product types; detailed shop-floor parameters are confidential and come from your plant walk, not from this guide.

## 1 Manufacturing footprint
- 12 manufacturing and assembly facilities across India, the USA, Mexico and China. Indian plants are the manufacturing plants; the US, Mexico and China facilities mainly do final assembly and finishing close to customers.
- Gurugram (Haryana) — the first plant (differential bevel gears from 1998) and Units II and III; differential assembly since 2008.
- Manesar (Haryana) — differential-assembly plant opened in 2019.
- Pune and Chakan (Maharashtra) — gear plants (Pune from 2005, second Chakan plant from 2023).
- Chennai (Tamil Nadu) — starter motors (conventional and micro-hybrid) and BLDC/PMSM traction motors; an SMT electronics line producing in-cabin radar sensor samples.
- Railway equipment plant(s) acquired in 2025.
- Michigan (USA, 2013), China (2016), Mexico (2017) and Silao, Mexico (2024) — assembly and finishing; the Mexico driveline plant won its first direct order in FY 2025-26.

## 2 Process flow
- Gears and differentials: incoming material inspection (special-steel alloy bars, steel blanks) → precision forging of near-net-shape bevel gears → machining → heat treatment → finish machining and grinding → washing → differential assembly (with castings, bolts and bearings) → end-of-line testing → packing → dispatch.
- Motors: incoming inspection of enamelled copper wire, aluminium die-castings, bearings, magnets and moulded parts → winding → rotor and stator assembly → final assembly with controller (for traction motors) → end-of-line electrical and performance testing → packing → dispatch.

## 3 Critical operations
Precision forging (gear tooth form), heat treatment (hardness and case depth), finish machining of special characteristics (bores, gear profiles) and end-of-line testing (gear noise/NVH for EV differentials, electrical performance for motors) are critical operations. They carry special characteristics marked on the drawing and are 100% controlled or controlled by SPC.

## 4 Traceability
Every part or batch carries a lot code linked to the material heat lot, machine, shift and test results. Traceability lets Quality contain a suspect lot precisely instead of sorting all stock.

## 5 Logistics
Customers call off parts through delivery schedules; plants ship against Advance Shipping Notices (ASN). Kanban is used for high-runner parts. Export customers are often served through the overseas assembly plants close to them. Customer-specific packaging standards are part of the PPAP.

## 6 Safety and quality mindset
- Safety: the company reports operations governed by ISO 45001 with HIRA (hazard identification and risk assessment), LOTO (lock-out/tag-out), regular facility audits, IoT monitoring and smart-camera surveillance, and mandatory DOJO safety training; FY 2025-26 had a zero lost-time injury frequency rate. Anyone may stop work that is unsafe.
- Quality: zero-defect mindset — do not accept, make or pass on a defect. Any operator may stop the line for a quality concern. The cost of poor quality multiplies at every step it travels toward the customer.

## 7 Materials and cost drivers (as reported)
Material cost was 47% of total income in FY 2025-26 (43% in FY 2024-25). Key inputs: special-steel alloy bars, iron castings, steel blanks and bolts (gears and differentials); enamelled copper wire, machined aluminium pressure die-castings, bearings, magnets and plastic moulded parts (motors); iron and aluminium castings, cables, elastomeric pads, reservoirs, anti-skid devices and speed sensors (railway). Rare-earth magnets are a reported supply risk for traction motors.
