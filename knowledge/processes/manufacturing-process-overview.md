# Manufacturing Process Overview

## 1 Process flow
Incoming material inspection → CNC machining → heat treatment → finish machining and grinding → washing → assembly → end-of-line testing (dimensional and leak testing) → packing → dispatch.

## 2 Critical operations
Heat treatment (hardness and case depth), finish machining of special characteristics (bores, gear profiles) and leak testing of cooling plates and housings are critical operations. They carry special characteristics marked on the drawing and are 100% controlled or controlled by SPC.

## 3 Traceability
Every part or batch carries a laser-marked lot code linked to the material heat lot, machine, shift and test results. Traceability lets Quality contain a suspect lot precisely instead of sorting all stock.

## 4 Logistics
Customers call off parts through delivery schedules; the plant ships against Advance Shipping Notices (ASN). Kanban is used for high-runner parts. Customer-specific packaging standards are part of the PPAP.

## 5 Safety mindset
Safety always comes first: personal protective equipment in all plant areas, lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) for machine intervention, and anyone may stop work that is unsafe.

## 6 Quality mindset
Zero-defect mindset: do not accept, make or pass on a defect. Any operator may stop the line for a quality concern. The cost of poor quality multiplies at every step it travels toward the customer.
