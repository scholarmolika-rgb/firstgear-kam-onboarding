# RFQ to Quotation SOP

## 1 Purpose
This standard operating procedure defines how a Request for Quotation (RFQ) from a customer is received, assessed, costed, approved and submitted. It applies to all new-part, re-sourcing and engineering-change RFQs handled by Key Account Managers (KAMs).

## 2 Scope and roles
The KAM owns the RFQ end to end as the single point of contact with the customer. Engineering owns technical feasibility, the Plant owns capacity and cycle-time inputs, SCM owns material and sourcing inputs, Quality owns special characteristics and validation effort, and Finance owns the cost model review.

## 3.1 RFQ intake
Every RFQ must be logged in the CRM within one working day of receipt and given an RFQ number. The KAM acknowledges receipt to the customer within two working days, confirms the submission deadline and lists any missing inputs (drawings, volumes, SOP date, packaging and logistics terms). An RFQ is not costed until the scope is clear: incomplete RFQs are returned to the customer with a written clarification list.

## 3.2 Feasibility assessment
After intake, the KAM convenes a cross-functional feasibility review. Engineering confirms technical feasibility and design-for-manufacturing risks, the Plant confirms capacity and expected cycle time, and SCM confirms material availability and sourcing lead times. The review ends with a documented go / no-go decision, normally within five working days. A "no-go" or a conditional feasibility must be explained to the customer before any price is discussed.

## 3.3 Cross-functional inputs
The quotation is built only from documented inputs: Engineering (design assumptions, DFM changes), Plant (cycle time, OEE, capacity), SCM (material specification, price basis, logistics), Quality (special characteristics, validation and PPAP effort) and Finance (overhead rates, margin targets). The KAM records the source and date of every input in the RFQ file.

## 3.4 Quotation workflow
The workflow is: cost model → pricing proposal → internal approval → customer submission. The cost model separates piece price, tooling and development cost. Quotations are valid for 90 days unless otherwise approved, and state the raw-material index basis used.

## 3.5 Approval chain
The KAM prepares the pricing proposal but does not approve it. The Head of Key Accounts approves quotations at or above the margin floor. Quotations below the margin floor, or with a lifetime contract value above ₹50 crore, require CFO approval. Strategic below-floor quotations also require the Managing Director. No quotation may be submitted, and no verbal price may be indicated to a customer, before the approval is recorded.

## 4 Records
The RFQ file holds the intake log, feasibility record, inputs, cost model, approval record and the submitted quotation.

## 5 Common failure modes
Quoting before feasibility; indicating verbal prices; using undocumented inputs; missing the raw-material index basis; submitting without recorded approval.
