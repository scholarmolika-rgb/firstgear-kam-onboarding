# Programme Governance — Nomination to SOP

## 1 Milestones
Nomination (business award) → programme kick-off → design freeze → tooling kick-off → off-tool samples → PPAP approval → SOP (start of production) → launch stabilisation (first 90 days).

## 2 KAM touchpoints
- Nomination: confirm the commercial basis (piece price, tooling, volumes, index basis) is recorded exactly as approved.
- Kick-off: confirm customer requirements and CSRs with NPD and Quality.
- Design freeze and changes: communicate customer engineering changes and their price and timing impact through the RFQ process.
- PPAP: track submission dates with Quality; never ship before approval or deviation.
- SOP and stabilisation: track launch issues at the weekly internal account review.

## 3 Engineering changes in programmes
Customer-initiated changes after design freeze are quoted (price and timing) before implementation. Supplier-initiated changes need customer approval and usually PPAP resubmission.

## 4 Complaint and 8D interface
During launch, complaints follow the Quality Governance Manual timelines. NPD supports Quality on launch-related root causes; the KAM owns customer communication.
