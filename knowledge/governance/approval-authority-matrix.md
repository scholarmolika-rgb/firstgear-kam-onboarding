# Commercial Approval Authority Matrix

## 1 Principle
Commercial commitments are approved by people, never by systems. Authority is personal, recorded and limited to the thresholds below. A KAM in onboarding has no pricing authority until the Reporting Boss records approval for guided pricing exposure.

## 2 Pricing
- KAM (after certification and Reporting Boss approval): prepares and proposes positions; may present an approved price.
- Head of Key Accounts (Reporting Boss): approves prices at or above the margin floor and price adjustments within ±2% of the last approved price.
- CFO: approves prices below the margin floor, any adjustment beyond ±2%, and contracts above ₹50 crore lifetime value.
- Managing Director: approves strategic below-floor pricing.

## 3 Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 60 days. Any change to payment terms requires CFO approval, regardless of value.

## 4 Tooling and development cost
Tooling waivers, amortisation into piece price beyond 3 years, or customer-funded tooling exceptions require CFO approval.

## 5 Delivery and quality commitments
The KAM may confirm to a customer only delivery dates confirmed in writing by SCM and the Plant. Quality commitments (containment, 8D dates) are confirmed jointly with Quality.

## 6 Records
Every approval is recorded with the approver, date, value and basis. Undocumented approvals are invalid.
